## Source [^1]
- A helicoid is a ruled surface which can be parametrized by$$ x(r,\theta) = r \cos(a\theta), \quad y(r,\theta) = r \sin(a\theta), z(r,\theta) = \theta. $$
- A curve of constant $r$ is a helix. Fixing $\theta = 0$ gives the $x$-axis; the helicoid is generated moving the $x$-axis up and down the $z$-axis, rotating the generating line at a constant angular speed $a$. The helicoid is, aside from the plane, the only ruled surface which is also a minimal surface.
