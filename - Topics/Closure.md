## Source[^2]
- The closure of a subset $A$ of a topological space is the smallest closed subset containing $A$, or equivalently is obtained by including in it all the limit points of $A$. For example, if $A$ is the set $\{x \in \mathbb{R}: 1 < x < 2\}$ then the closure of $A$ would include $1$ and $2$ as the limit points, giving $\{x \in \mathbb{R}: 1 \le x \le 2\}$. See DENSE SET, KURATOWSKI CLOSURE AXIOMS.
