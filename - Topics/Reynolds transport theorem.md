## Source [^1]
- For a region of fluid $V(t)$ moving in a flow velocity $\mathbf{u}$ and a differentiable function $G(\mathbf{x},t)$ defined on $V(t)$,$$\frac{\text{d}}{\text{dt}} \int_{V(t)} G \, \text{d}V = \int_{V(t)} \left( \frac{\text{D}G}{\text{Dt}} + G \nabla \cdot \mathbf{u} \right) \text{d}V$$where $\text{D}/\text{Dt}$ denotes the convective derivative. This can be viewed as a generalization of Leibniz's integral rule.
