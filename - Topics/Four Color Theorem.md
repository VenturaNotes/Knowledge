## Source [^1]
- It had long been observed that any geographical map (that is, a division of the plane into regions) could be coloured with just four colours in such a way that no two neighbouring regions had the same colour. A proof of this, the Four Colour Theorem, was sought by mathematicians from about the $1850\text{s}$ In $1890$ Heawood proved that five colours would suffice, but it was not until $1976$ that Appel and Haken proved the Four Color Theorem itself. Initially, some mathematicians were sceptical of the proof because it relied on a massive amount of checking of configurations by computer that could not easily be verified independently. However, the proof is now generally accepted.
