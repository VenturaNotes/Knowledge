## Source [^1]
- One of the earliest mathematical journals, particularly as one not being solely the proceedings of some academy or learned society. It was begun by August Crelle in Berlin in 1826 and published many noteworthy papers, including Abel’s proof that the general quintic equation is not solvable by radicals.
