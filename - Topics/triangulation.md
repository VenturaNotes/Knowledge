## Source [^1]
- A collection of triangles such that any pair of triangles intersect at most at one common vertex or along a common edge, whose union describes a surface in space.
## Source[^2]
### In Geometry
- The method of fixing a point by using directions from two known points to construct a triangle.
### In Topology
- A triangulation of a surface is a subdivision in which all the faces are triangles (in the sense of having three vertices and three curves as edges). See SIMPLICIAL COMPLEX.
