## Source [^1]
- In $\mathbb{R}^n$, the norm defined by$$ \| \mathbf{x} \|_p = \left( \sum_{i=1}^n |x_i|^p \right)^{\frac{1}{p}}. $$for $1 \le p \le \infty$. Note that when $p = 2$, this is just the standard Euclidean norm, or the length of the vector, and for $p = 1$ it is the Manhattan or taxicab norm. See $\mathbb{P}$.
