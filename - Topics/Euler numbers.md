## Source [^1]
- An integer sequence $E_n$, related to the Bernoulli numbers, and defined by the generating function$$\frac{1}{\cosh x} = \sum_{n=0}^\infty \frac{E_n x^n}{n!}.$$
- As $\cosh x$ is an even function, all odd Euler numbers are zero. The first few even Euler numbers are $E_0 = 1, E_2 = -1, E_4 = 5, E_6 = -61, E_8 = 1385$.
