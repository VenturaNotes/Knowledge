## Source [^1]
- The force experienced by a charged particle in an electromagnetic field. The law states that a particle with charge $q$, moving with a velocity $\mathbf{v}$ through an electric field $\mathbf{E}$ and a magnetic field $\mathbf{B}$, will experience a force $\mathbf{F}$ given by $\mathbf{F} = q(\mathbf{E} + \mathbf{v} \times \mathbf{B})$.
