## Source [^1]
- Any connected closed (i.e. compact) surface is homeomorphic to precisely one of a list of standard surfaces. The orientable standard surfaces are the tori of differing genus $g \ge 0$; the non-orientable standard surfaces are the sphere with $k > 0$ Möbius strips sewn in. The standard surfaces are topologically distinct on account of the orientability (or not) and their Euler characteristic.
