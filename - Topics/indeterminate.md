## Source [^1]
- A variable, in particular a formal variable, such as in the polynomial ring $F[x]$ or the formal power series ring $F[[x]]$ over a field $F$. Such polynomials might be formally differentiated, but without reference to limits, and such power series multiplied, but without reference to convergence.
