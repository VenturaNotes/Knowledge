## Source [^1]
- A set of points $(x, y, z)$ in Cartesian space, represented as a graph as $z=f(x, y)$, or defined implicitly as $g(x, y, z) = 0$, or described as a parameterized surface. See also ATLAS, CHART, MANIFOLD.
### In Topology
- A 2-dimensional manifold. The most familiar examples are the surfaces of solids, but examples like the non-orientable Klein bottle and the real projective plane are also surfaces. See CLASSIFICATION THEOREM FOR SURFACES, MANIFOLD.
### Of a Solid
- (of a solid) The boundary of a three-dimensional geometric shape. For example, the surface of a solid cylinder comprises two discs and a curved surface.
