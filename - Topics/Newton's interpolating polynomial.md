## Source [^1]
- The polynomial of degree $n$, which interpolates $n+1$ evenly spaced data points $(x_0, y_0), (x_1, y_1), \dots, (x_n, y_n)$, where $x_k = x_0 + kh$, is given by$$y_0 + \frac{(x - x_0)}{h} \Delta y_0 + \frac{(x - x_0)(x - x_1)}{2!h^2} \Delta^2 y_0 + \dots + \frac{(x - x_0) \dots (x - x_n)}{n!h^n} \Delta^n y_0$$where $\Delta y_0 = y_1 - y_0, \Delta^2 y_0 = \Delta y_1 - \Delta y_0$, etc. which are obtained by constructing a forward difference table.
