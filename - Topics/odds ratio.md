## Source [^1]
- a measure of the association between an exposure and an outcome, calculated by comparing the odds of the outcome of interest in those who are given and those who are not given exposure with the outcome of interest. It is most commonly used in case control studies.
## Source[^2]
- Defined as the ratio of the odds of an event happening in two distinct groups. If the probabilities of the event in the two groups are $p$ and $q$ then the odds are $p : (1-p)$ and $q : (1-q)$ and the odds ratio is $\frac{p/(1-p)}{q/(1-q)} = \frac{p(1-q)}{q(1-p)}$.
