## Source [^1]
- A non-singular curve in the complex projective plane (see PROJECTIVE SPACE) defined by a degree $d$ equation is a Riemann surface of genus$$g = \frac{1}{2}(d - 1)(d - 2).$$
- If $d = 1$ or 2, then $g = 0$, and the curve gives the Riemann sphere; and if $d = 3$, then $g = 1$, an elliptic curve which is topologically a torus.
