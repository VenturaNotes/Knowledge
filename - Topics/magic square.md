## Source [^1]
- A square array of numbers arranged in rows and columns in such a way that all the rows, all the columns, and both diagonals add up to the same total. Often the numbers used are $1, 2, \dots, n^2$, where $n$ is the number of rows and columns. The example on the left in the figure is said to be of ancient Chinese origin; that on the right is featured in an engraving by Albrecht Dürer.
