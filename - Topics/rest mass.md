## Source [^1]
- In special relativity, the mass of a particle as measured in its rest frame. A particle of rest mass $m_0$ has an inertial mass (or relativistic mass) of $m_0/\sqrt{1 - v^2/c^2}$ to an observer travelling at speed $v$ relative to the particle. (Here $c$ denotes the speed of light.)
