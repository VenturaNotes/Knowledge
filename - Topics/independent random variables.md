## Source [^1]
- Two discrete random variables $X$ and $Y$ are independent if $X = x$ and $Y = y$ are independent events for all $x$ and $y$; that is, $\text{Pr}(X=x, Y=y) = \text{Pr}(X=x) \text{Pr}(Y=y)$. Two continuous random variables $X$ and $Y$, with joint probability density function $f(x,y)$, are independent if $f(x,y) = f_1(x) f_2(y)$, where $f_1$ and $f_2$ are the marginal probability density functions.
