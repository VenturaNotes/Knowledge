## Source [^1]
- A parameter associated with the behavior of two bodies during a collision. Suppose that two billiard balls are traveling in the same straight line and have velocities $u_1$ and $u_2$ before the collision and velocities $v_1$ and $v_2$ after the collision. If the coefficient of restitution is $e$, then$$v_2 - v_1 = -e(u_2 - u_1).$$This formula is Newton’s law of restitution. The coefficient of restitution always satisfies $0 \le e \le 1$. When $e = 0$, the balls remain in contact after the perfectly inelastic collision. When $e = 1$, the collision is perfectly elastic: there is no loss of kinetic energy.
