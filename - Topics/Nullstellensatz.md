## Source [^1]
- The following important result in commutative algebra due to Hilbert. Let $F$ be an algebraically closed field (such as $\mathbb{C}$) and $n$ be a positive integer. For an ideal $J$ in the polynomial ring $F[x_1, \dots, x_n]$ and for a subset $S$ of $F^n$ we may define$$V(J) = \{x \in F^n ; |f(x) = 0 \text{ for all } f \in J\}; \quad I(S) = \{f \in F[x_1, \dots, x_n] ; |f(x) = 0 \text{ for all } x \in S\}.$$
- Then $I(V(J)) = \sqrt{J}$, the radical of $J$. By comparison $V(I(S)) = \overline{S}$, the closure of $S$ in the Zariski topology.
