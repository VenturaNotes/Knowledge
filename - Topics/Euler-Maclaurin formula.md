## Source [^1]
- Let $a < b$ be integers and $f(x)$ be a function on $[a, b]$ with derivatives of all orders. Let $I = \int_a^b f(x) \, dx$ and let $S$ denote the estimate applying the trapezium rule to this integral using unit intervals. The Euler-Maclaurin formula then states$$S - I = \sum_{k=1}^\infty \frac{B_{2k}}{(2k)!} \left( f^{(2k-1)}(b) - f^{(2k-1)}(a) \right),$$where $B_n$ denotes the $n\text{th}$ Bernoulli number, and provided that$$\lim_{n \to \infty} \frac{1}{(2\pi)^n} \int_a^b |f^{(n)}(x)| \, dx = 0.$$
