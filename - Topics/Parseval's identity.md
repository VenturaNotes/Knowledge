## Source [^1]
- The identity states that$$ \|x\|^2 = \sum_{i=1}^n |\langle x, x_i \rangle|^2 $$for a orthonormal basis $x_1, x_2, \dots, x_n$ of a finite-dimensional inner product space $X$ and $x \in X$. This is a generalization of Pythagoras’ Theorem. The identity holds for an orthonormal sequence in an infinite-dimensional Hilbert space if and only if the sequence is complete, or equivalently, a Schauder basis. Compare BESSEL’S INEQUALITY.
