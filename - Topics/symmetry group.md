## Source [^1]
- A group consisting of all those functions that transform a rigid plane figure into itself; the dyadic operation on the elements of the group is that of composition of functions. The larger and more complex a symmetry group the greater the symmetry associated with the underlying geometric figure.
## Source[^2]
- (of a geometrical figure) The symmetries of a figure form its symmetry group under composition. The symmetry group of a regular polygon is a dihedral group, of a rectangle is $V_4$, of the tetrahedron is $A_4$, of the cube and octahedron is $S_4$, and of the dodecahedron and icosahedron is $A_5$.
