## Source [^1]
- (mixed boundary condition) A combination of the function and the flux at the boundary. For example, a function $\varphi$ might satisfy Laplace's equation inside a region and the Robin boundary condition $c \varphi + \partial \varphi / \partial n = f$ on the boundary where $c$ is a constant and $f$ a specified function; here $\partial \varphi / \partial n$ denotes the directional derivative of $\varphi$ in the direction of the outward pointing normal. The existence and uniqueness of any solution depends on the sign of $c$. Compare DIRICHLET PROBLEM, NEUMANN CONDITION.
