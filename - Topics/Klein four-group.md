## Source [^1]
- Essentially (i.e. up to isomorphism), there are two groups with four elements. One is cyclic. The other is the Klein four-group, often denoted $V_4$. It is abelian and together with the identity element has three commuting order two elements. It is isomorphic to the product group $C_2 \times C_2$.
