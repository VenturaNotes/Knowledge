## Source [^1]
- A finite measure space $(\Omega, \mathcal{F}, P)$ with total measure $1$ — that is $P(\Omega) = 1$. The set $\Omega$ is the sample space, elements of the sigma algebra $\mathcal{F}$ are called events, and $P$ is the probability measure.
