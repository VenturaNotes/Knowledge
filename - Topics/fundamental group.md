## Source [^1]
- A topological invariant important in algebraic topology and the first of the homotopy groups. For a path-connected space $X$ with base point $x_0$, the fundamental group $\pi_1(X)$ is the set of equivalence classes of continuous loops $\gamma: [0, 1] \to X$ such that $\gamma(0) = \gamma(1) = x_0$, and where two loops are equivalent if they are homotopic. The product $\gamma_1 * \gamma_2$ of two loops is the loop $\gamma_2$ followed by $\gamma_1$ and the inverse of $\gamma$ is the loop in reverse. A continuous function $f: X \to Y$ induces a homomorphism $f_*: \pi_1(X) \to \pi_1(Y)$ by sending $\gamma$ to $f(\gamma)$. The circle's fundamental group is $\mathbb{Z}$; the knot group of a knot is the fundamental group of its complement.
