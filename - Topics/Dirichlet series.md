## Source [^1]
- A series in the form $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} a_n e^{-\lambda_n z}$ where $a_n$ and $z$ are complex and $\{\lambda_n\}$ is a monotonic increasing sequence of real numbers. When $\lambda_n = \ln n$, the series reduces to $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} a_n n^{-z},$ known as a Dirichlet L-series.
