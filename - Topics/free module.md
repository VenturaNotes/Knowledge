## Source [^1]
- A module with a basis. Unlike vector spaces, modules typically do not have bases. A free module over a ring $R$ is isomorphic to $R^n$ for some $n$, and all bases of $R^n$ have $n$ elements. Note $\mathbb{Z}_2$ is free as a $\mathbb{Z}_2$-module, as $\{1\}$ is a basis, but not free as a $\mathbb{Z}$-module, as $1$ is a torsion element.
