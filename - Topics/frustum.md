## Source [^1]
- (frusta) A frustum of a right-circular cone is the part between two parallel planes perpendicular to the axis. Suppose that the planes are a distance $h$ apart and that the circles that form the top and bottom of the frustum have radii $a$ and $b$. Then the volume of the frustum equals $\frac{1}{3}\pi h(a^2 + ab + b^2)$.
- Let $l$ be the slant height of the frustum; that is, the length of the part of a generator between the top and bottom of the frustum. Then the area of the curved surface of the frustum equals $\pi(a + b)l$.
