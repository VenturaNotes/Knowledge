## Source [^1]
- (1629–95) Dutch mathematician, astronomer, and physicist. In mathematics, he is remembered for his work on pendulum clocks and his contributions in the field of dynamics. These concerned, for example, the period of oscillation of a simple pendulum and matters such as the centrifugal force in uniform circular motion.
