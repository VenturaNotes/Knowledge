## Source [^1]
- (1768-1830) French engineer and mathematician, best known in mathematics for his fundamental contributions to the theory of heat conduction and his study of trigonometric series. These so-called Fourier series are of immense importance in physics, engineering and other disciplines, as well as being of great mathematical interest.
