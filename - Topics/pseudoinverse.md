## Source [^1]
- A generalization of the notion of the inverse of a square matrix to other matrices. The pseudoinverse $\mathbf{A}^+$ of a matrix $\mathbf{A}$ is characterized by the properties
	- (i) $\mathbf{AA}^+\mathbf{A} = \mathbf{A}$ and $\mathbf{A}^+\mathbf{AA}^+ = \mathbf{A}^+$
	- (ii) $\mathbf{AA}^+$ and $\mathbf{A}^+\mathbf{A}$ are symmetric.
- When $\mathbf{A}$ is invertible, $\mathbf{A}^+ = \mathbf{A}^{-1}$. Further, $\mathbf{x}_0 = \mathbf{A}^+\mathbf{b}$ minimizes $|\mathbf{Ax}-\mathbf{b}|$ as $\mathbf{x}$ varies. See SINGULAR VALUE DECOMPOSITION.
