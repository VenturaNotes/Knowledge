## Source[^2]
- $n$. one of the faculties by which the qualities of the external environment are appreciated $\textendash$ sight, hearing, smell, taste, or touch.
## Source[^3]
- (sense-preserving) One of the two possible orientations of an angle, i.e. clockwise or anticlockwise. A map is sense-preserving if it maintains the sense of angles. So, a rotation is sense-preserving and a reflection is sense-reversing. A square matrix $A$ represents a sense-preserving linear map if $\det A > 0$ and a sense-reversing map if $\det A < 0$. See also POSITIVELY ORIENTED, RIGHT-HANDED SYSTEM.
