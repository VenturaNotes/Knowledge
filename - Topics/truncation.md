## Source [^1]
- Suppose that a number has more digits than can be conveniently handled or stored. In truncation (as opposed to rounding), the extra digits are simply dropped; for example, when truncated to 1 decimal place, the numbers 1.875 and 1.845 both become 1.8. See also DECIMAL PLACES, SIGNIFICANT FIGURES.
