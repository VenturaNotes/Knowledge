## Source [^1]
- adj. In relation to a disorder, referring to changes in behavior or action, without any observable change in physical structure in the person or any organ or body part.
## Source[^2]
- The term, ‘functional’ is variously used, typically referring to a scalar-valued function on a set. In calculus of variations, the aim is to minimize a functional which might represent length, area, work, etc. In linear algebra and functional analysis, the term is used for a linear map $\varphi: V \to F$ from a vector space to its base field, and so an element of the dual space.
