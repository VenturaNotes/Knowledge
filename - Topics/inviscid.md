## Source [^1]
- Not viscous. The force exerted across a small surface element within an inviscid fluid is $p\mathbf{n}dS$, where $p$ denotes pressure, $\mathbf{n}$ is the unit normal, and $dS$ is the surface area of the element. In a viscous fluid there would be tangential components to this contact force due to friction. See EULER'S EQUATIONS, NAVIER-STOKES EQUATIONS.
