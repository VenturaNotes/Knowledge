## Source [^1]
- From the definition of multiplication (of a complex number), it follows that $(\cos\theta_1 + i\sin\theta_1)(\cos\theta_2 + i\sin\theta_2) = \cos(\theta_1 + \theta_2) + i\sin(\theta_1 + \theta_2)$. This leads to the following result known as De Moivre's Theorem, which is crucial to calculating the powers $z^n$ of a complex number $z$:
- Theorem
	- For any integer $n, (\cos\theta + i\sin\theta)^n = \cos n\theta + i\sin n\theta$.
