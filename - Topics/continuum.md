## Source [^1]
- $n$. A continuous sequence or dimension in which adjacent points are not noticeably different but the extremes are clearly distinct. \[From Latin continuus uninterrupted, from continere to hang together, from com together + tenere to hold]
## Source[^2]
- A somewhat archaic word for the set of real numbers. Also, in general topology, a non-empty compact, connected, Hausdorff space.
