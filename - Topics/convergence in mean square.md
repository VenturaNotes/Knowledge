## Source [^1]
- A sequence of random variables $X_n$ converges in mean square to a random variable $X$ if $\text{E}(|X_n - X|^2) \to 0$ as $n \to \infty$, where $\text{E}$ denotes expectation. This is written $X_n \xrightarrow{L^2} X$. Convergence in mean square implies convergence in probability.
