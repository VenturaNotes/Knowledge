## Source [^1]
- (of a relation) Not symmetric, or asymmetric. The relation has to hold for some pairs in both orders and hold for only one order for some other pairs, i.e. there exist elements $a, b, c, d$ for which $a \sim b, b \sim a$, whereas $c \sim d, d \sim c$ does not hold.
