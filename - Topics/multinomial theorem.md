## Source [^1]
- A generalization of the binomial theorem, giving the expansions of positive integral powers of a multinomial expression$$ (x_1 + x_2 + \dots + x_k)^n = \sum \left( \frac{n!}{n_1! n_2! \dots n_k!} x_1^{n_1} x_2^{n_2} \dots x_k^{n_k} \right) $$where the sum is over all $k$-tuples $n_1, n_2, \dots, n_k$ of non-negative integers which total $n$.
