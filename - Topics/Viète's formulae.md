## Source [^1]
- Say that a cubic $z^3 + az^2 + bz + c$ has roots $\alpha, \beta, \gamma$ so that$$z^3 + az^2 + bz + c = (z - \alpha)(z - \beta)(z - \gamma),$$then, by expanding the RHS and comparing coefficients,$$\alpha + \beta + \gamma = -a, \quad \alpha\beta + \beta\gamma + \gamma\alpha = b, \quad \alpha\beta\gamma = -c.$$
- These are Viète’s formulae and naturally generalize to higher degree polynomials.
