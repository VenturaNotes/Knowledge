## Source [^1]
- A continuous function $f : U \to \mathbb{C}$ on an open set $U$ of $\mathbb{C}$, such that $\int_C f(z) \, dz = 0$ for all closed curves $C$ in $U$, is holomorphic. This is a partial converse of Cauchy’s integral theorem.
