## Source [^1]
- (Cauchy's test) For a series $a_1 + a_2 + a_3 + \dots$ of positive terms:
	- if $\limsup a_n^{1/n} < 1$, then the series converges.
	- if $\limsup a_n^{1/n} > 1$, then the series diverges.
	- if $\limsup a_n^{1/n} = 1$, then the series may or may not converge.
- See also RADIUS OF CONVERGENCE.
