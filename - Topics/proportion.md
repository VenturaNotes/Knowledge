## Source [^1]
- The portion of a set with a certain characteristic. This might be expressed as a fraction or percentage of the whole.
### Proportional
- (proportional) If two quantities $x$ and $y$ are related by an equation $y = kx$, where $k$ is a constant, then $y$ is said to be (directly) proportional to $x$, which may be written $y \propto x$. The constant $k$ is the constant of proportionality. It is also said that $y$ varies directly as $x$. When $y$ is plotted against $x$, the graph is a straight line through the origin.
- If $y = k/x$, then $y$ is inversely proportional to $x$. This is written $y \propto 1/x$, and it is said that $y$ varies inversely as $x$.
