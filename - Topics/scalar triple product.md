- The scalar triple product of three vectors $\mathbf{a, b,}$ and $\mathbf{c},$ is $\mathbf{a} \cdot (\mathbf{b} \times \mathbf{c}),$ the scalar product of $\mathbf{a}$ with the vector product $\mathbf{b} \times \mathbf{c}$. It is a scalar quantity and is denoted $[\mathbf{a, b, c}]$. It has the following properties:
	- (i) $[\mathbf{a, b, c}] = -[\mathbf{a, c, b}]$.
	- (ii) $[\mathbf{a, b, c}] = [\mathbf{b, c, a}] = [\mathbf{c, a, b}]$.
	- (iii) The vectors $\mathbf{a, b,}$ and $\mathbf{c}$ are linearly dependent if and only if $[\mathbf{a, b, c}] = 0$.
	- (iv) If $\mathbf{a} = (a_1, a_2, a_3)$, etc., then
		- $$\begin{align}[\mathbf{a, b, c}] &= a_1(b_2c_3 - b_3c_2) + a_2(b_3c_1 - b_1c_3) + a_3(b_1c_2 - b_2c_1)\\&= \begin{vmatrix} a_1 & a_2 & a_3 \\ b_1 & b_2 & b_3 \\ c_1 & c_2 & c_3 \end{vmatrix}\end{align}$$
	- (v) Let $\vec{OA}, \vec{OB},$ and $\vec{OC}$ represent $\mathbf{a, b}$ and $\mathbf{c}$. Then the parallelepiped with $OA, OB,$ and $OC$ as three of its edges has volume equal to the absolute value of $[\mathbf{a, b, c}]$.
