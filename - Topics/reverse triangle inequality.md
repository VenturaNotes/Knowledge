## Source [^1]
- For vectors (or real or complex numbers) $\mathbf{x,y}$, the inequality $|\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{y}| \leq | |\mathbf{x}| - |\mathbf{y}| |$. This is an easy consequence of the triangle inequality. The inequality also holds in metric spaces, as $|d(b,a)-d(a,c)| \leq d(b,c)$, where $d$ denotes the metric.
