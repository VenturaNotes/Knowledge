## Source [^1]
- (of a metric space) Relating to a metric space which, for any $\varepsilon > 0$, can be presented as the union of finite many balls of radius $\varepsilon$. A subspace of a totally bounded metric space is totally bounded. A metric space is compact if and only if it is complete and totally bounded.
