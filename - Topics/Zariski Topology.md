## Synthesis
- A topology on algebraic sets where closed sets are precisely the solution sets of polynomials
## Source [^1]
- An important, but not Hausdorff, topology in algebraic geometry. In the Zariski topology on $\mathbb{C}^n$ a closed subset is one of the form$$V(I) = \{x | f(x) = 0 \text{ for all } f \in I\},$$where $I \subseteq \mathbb{C}[x_1, x_2, \dots, x_n]$. The Zariski topology on complex projective space can be similarly defined using homogeneous polynomials. The Zariski topology on any affine or projective variety is then the subspace topology induced by the ambient space.
