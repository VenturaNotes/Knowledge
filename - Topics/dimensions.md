## Source [^1]
- Many physical quantities can be described in terms of the basic dimensions of mass $M$, length $L$, and time $T$, using positive and negative indices. For example, the following have the dimensions given: area, $L^2$; velocity, $LT^{-1}$; force, $MLT^{-2}$; linear momentum, $MLT^{-1}$; energy, $ML^2T^{-2}$; and power, $ML^2T^{-3}$. Temperature is another dimension, and there are others (e.g. electric current or luminous intensity) within the SI units system, but these are more relevant to physics than mathematics.
