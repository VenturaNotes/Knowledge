## Source [^1]
- For a field $F$ with prime characteristic $p$, the Frobenius endomorphism is the map $\varphi: F \to F$ defined by $\varphi(r) = r^p$. Note $\varphi$ is clearly multiplicative and is additive as the binomial coefficient $\binom{p}{k}$ is divisible by $p$ for $0 < k < p$. By Fermat's Little Theorem, $\varphi$ fixes the prime subfield. If $F$ is finite, then $\varphi$ is an automorphism and generates the Galois group of $F$ over the prime subfield.
