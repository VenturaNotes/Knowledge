## Source [^1]
- A theorem which determines the number of spanning trees in a graph. Let $\mathbf{A}$ be the adjacency matrix of a graph $G$ with $n$ vertices and let $\mathbf{L} = \mathbf{D} - \mathbf{A}$ where $\mathbf{D}$ is the diagonal matrix whose entries are the degrees of the vertices. Then the number of spanning trees equals $c_1/n$, where $c_1$ is the coefficient of $x$ in the characteristic polynomial $\chi_{\mathbf{L}}(x)$.
