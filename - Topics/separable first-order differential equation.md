## Source [^1]
- A first-order differential equation $dy/dx = f(x,y)$ in which the function $f$ can be expressed as the product of a function of $x$ and a function of $y$. The differential equation then has the form $dy/dx = g(x)h(y)$, and its solution is given by the equation$$\int \frac{1}{h(y)} dy = \int g(x) dx + c,$$where $c$ is an arbitrary constant.
