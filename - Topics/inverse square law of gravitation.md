## Source [^1]
- The following law, due to Newton, that describes the force of attraction that exists between two particles. Say particles $P$ and $S$ have gravitational masses $m$ and $M$, respectively. Let $\mathbf{r} = \mathbf{SP} \to$ be the position vector of $P$ relative to $S$ and let $r = |\mathbf{r}|$. Then the force $\mathbf{F}$ experienced by $P$ is given by$$\mathbf{F} = -\frac{GMm}{r^3}\mathbf{r}$$where $G$ is the gravitational constant. Since $\mathbf{r}/r$ is a unit vector, the magnitude of the gravitational force experienced by $P$ is inversely proportional to the square of the distance between $S$ and $P$. The force experienced by $S$ is equal and opposite to that experienced by $P$.
