## Source [^1]
- (Liouville’s constant) Irrational number $x$ with the property that for every integer $n$, there is a rational number $\frac{p}{q}$ such that $|x - \frac{p}{q}| < \frac{1}{q^n}$. All Liouville numbers are transcendental numbers. One such is Liouville’s constant$$\sum_{k=1}^\infty 10^{-k!}$$
