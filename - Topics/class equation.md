## Source [^1]
- For a finite group $G$, the class equation states that$$|G| = |Z(G)| + \sum |C_k|$$where $Z(G)$ denotes the center of $G$ and the $C_k$ are the remaining conjugacy classes. The equation follows from the fact that the conjugacy classes partition $G$ and that a group element has a singleton conjugacy class if and only if it is in the center.
