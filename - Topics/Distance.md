## Source [^1]
- (in a graph) The distance between two vertices in a graph is the length of the shortest path between them. This defines a metric on the set of vertices.
---
- (in the complex plane) If $P_1$ and $P_2$ are points represented by complex numbers $z_1$ and $z_2$, the distance $|P_1 P_2|$ is equal to $|z_1 - z_2|$, the modulus of $z_1 - z_2$.
