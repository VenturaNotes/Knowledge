## Source [^1]
- A set of polynomial solutions $\{P_n(x)\}$ to Legendre’s differential equation are generated by the expansion of$$\frac{1}{\sqrt{(1 - 2xt + t^2)}}$$as the coefficients of $t^n$.
- Alternatively they can be found by Rodrigues’ formula$$P_n(x) = \frac{1}{2^n n!} \frac{d^n}{dx^n}(x^2 - 1)^n.$$
