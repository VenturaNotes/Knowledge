## Source [^1]
- The Navier-Stokes equations for the flow of a viscous incompressible fluid with flow velocity $\mathbf{u}$ state$$\text{div}\mathbf{u}=0, \quad \frac{\text{D}\mathbf{u}}{\text{D}t} = -\frac{1}{\rho}\nabla p + \nu\nabla^2\mathbf{u} + \mathbf{g}.$$Here $\rho$ denotes the (constant) density, $p$ denotes pressure, $\nu$ denotes the constant viscosity, $\mathbf{g}$ denotes acceleration due to gravity, and $\text{D}/\text{D}t$ is the convective derivative. Note that if $\nu = 0$, then the Navier-Stokes equations become Euler's equations.
