## Source [^1]
- ($1905\text{-}90$) German mathematician who became one of the most important figures in mathematics education. He founded the Institute for the Development of Mathematical Education in Utrecht in $1971$ and was its first director. It was renamed the Freudenthal Institute in $1991$ after his death.
