## Source [^1]
- In the real world, the passage of time is a universal experience which clocks of various kinds have been designed to measure. In a mathematical model, time is represented by a real variable, usually denoted by $t$, with $t = 0$ corresponding to some suitable starting point. An observer associated with a frame of reference has the capability to measure the duration of time intervals between events occurring in the problem being investigated.
- Time has the dimension $\text{T}$, and the SI unit of measurement is the second.
- See SIMULTANEITY, TIME DILATION.
