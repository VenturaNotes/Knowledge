- (in trigonometry) A formula in trigonometry that expresses a function of a double angle in terms of the single angle. This can be obtained from the corresponding compound angle formulae by substituting $A = B = x$:
$$ \begin{aligned} \sin(2x) &= 2 \sin x \cos x \\ \cos(2x) &= \cos^2 x - \sin^2 x \\ \tan(2x) &= \frac{2 \tan x}{1 - \tan^2 x}. \end{aligned}$$
