## Source [^1]
- A linear map $N: V \to V$ of an inner product space such that $NN^* = N^*N$ where $N^*$ denotes the adjoint of $N$. Self-adjoint and unitary maps (see UNITARY MATRIX) are normal. The spectral theorem applies to normal operators.
