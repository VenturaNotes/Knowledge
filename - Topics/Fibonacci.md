## Source [^1]
- (about 1170-1250) Pseudonym of an Italian merchant by the name of Leonardo of Pisa, who helped introduce the Hindu-Arabic number system to Europe. He strongly advocated this system in Liber abaci, published in 1202, which also contained problems including one that gives rise to the Fibonacci numbers.
