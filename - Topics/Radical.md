## Source[^2]
- Given an ideal $I$ in a ring $R$, its radical is$$\sqrt{I} = \{r \in R | r^n \in I \text{ for some } n\}.$$
- This is an ideal of $R$ which contains $I$. A radical ideal is an ideal which equals its radical. Prime ideals are radical; indeed, $\sqrt{I}$ equals the intersection of all prime ideals containing $I$.
- See also NILRADICAL.
