## Source [^1]
- The symbol $+$ used to denote addition of numbers or any other binary operation with similar properties, for example in rings and abelian groups. It can also be used to denote a positive quantity, though the absence of $+$ or $-$, as in $\sqrt{2}$ is taken to imply the positive.
