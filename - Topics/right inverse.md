## Source [^1]
- A right inverse $g : Y \rightarrow X$ of a map $f : X \rightarrow Y$ satisfies $f(g(y)) = y$ for all $y$ in $Y$. The existence of a right inverse is equivalent to $f$ being onto, assuming the axiom of choice. See INVERSE FUNCTION, LEFT INVERSE.
