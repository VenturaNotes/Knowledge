## Source [^1]
- A complex number $z$ such that $z^3 = 1$. The three cube roots of unity are 1, $\omega$ and $\omega^2$, where$$\begin{aligned} \omega &= e^{2\pi i/3} = \cos \frac{2\pi}{3} + i \sin \frac{2\pi}{3} \\ &= -\frac{1}{2} + \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}i, \end{aligned}$$$$\begin{aligned} \omega^2 &= e^{4\pi i/3} = \cos \frac{4\pi}{3} + i \sin \frac{4\pi}{3} \\ &= -\frac{1}{2} - \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}i. \end{aligned}$$
- Properties: (i) $\omega^2 = \bar{\omega}$ (see CONJUGATE), (ii) $1 + \omega + \omega^2 = 0$.
