## Source [^1]
- The representation of a number to base $16$. In this system, $0\text{--}9$ and $A\text{--}F$ are used as the digits, with $A\text{--}F$ representing the numbers $10\text{--}15$. For example, the hexadecimal representation of $712$ is$$ 712 = 2 \times 16^2 + 12 \times 16 + 8 = 2\text{C}8_{16}. $$
- Hexadecimal notation is important in computing; it translates easily into binary notation but is more concise and easier to read.
