## Source [^1]
- (1903–30) Despite dying tragically young, Ramsey was influential in mathematics, philosophy, and economics. Ramsey's Theorem states that there is a number $R(m,n)$ such that every colouring with two colours of the edges of a complete graph with $R(m,n)$ vertices has a collection of $m$ vertices with all edges between them being the first colour or a similar collection with $n$ vertices for the second colour. In practice, these Ramsey numbers are difficult to calculate, even for small values of $m$ and $n$.
