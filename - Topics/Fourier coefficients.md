## Source [^1]
- Given $L > 0$, the coefficients $a_n$ and $b_n$, in the Fourier series representation of a function of period $2L$, are given by$$\begin{align}&a_n = \frac{1}{L} \int_{-L}^{L} f(x) \cos\left(\frac{n\pi x}{L}\right) \text{ dx}\\&b_n = \frac{1}{L} \int_{-L}^{L} f(x) \sin\left(\frac{n\pi x}{L}\right) \text{ dx}.\end{align}$$
