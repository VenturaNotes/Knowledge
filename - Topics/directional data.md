## Source [^1]
- (directional statistics) Also known as cyclic data, or circular data, such data are associated, for example, with direction of travel, time of day, and day of the year. The data must not be treated linearly; it must instead be recognized that a bearing of $359^\circ$ is close to a bearing of $1^\circ$, or that $23.59$ is approximately $00.01$ (in the 24-hour clock). As such, the data are treated as being distributed on a circle (or sphere).
