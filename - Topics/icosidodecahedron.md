## Source [^1]
- One of the Archimedean solids, with 12 pentagonal faces and 20 triangular faces. It can be formed by cutting off the corners of a dodecahedron to obtain a polyhedron whose vertices lie at the midpoints of the edges of the original dodecahedron.
