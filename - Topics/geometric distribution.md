## Source [^1]
- A discrete distribution with a probability function of the form$$P[X=x \mid p]=p(1-p)^{x-1}$$for $x=1,2, \ldots$ and $0 \leq p \leq 1$. The geometric distribution has mean $1 / p$ and variance $(1-p) / p^{2}$.
## Source[^2]
- The discrete probability distribution for the number of experiments required to achieve the first success in a sequence of independent experiments, all with the same probability $p$ of success. The probability mass function is given by $\Pr(X = r) = p(1-p)^{r-1}$, for $r = 1, 2, \dots$ It has mean $1/p$ and variance $(1-p)/p^2$.
