## Source [^1]
- Let $f(x)$ be a non-negative, continuous, decreasing function defined for $x \ge N$, where $N$ is an integer. Then, the series $\sum_{n=N}^\infty f(n)$ converges if and only if the integral $\int_N^\infty f(x) \, dx$ converges. For example, setting $N = 1$ and $f(x) = 1/x$ shows that the harmonic series diverges as $\int_1^X \frac{dx}{x} = \ln X$ tends to infinity as $X$ tends to infinity.
