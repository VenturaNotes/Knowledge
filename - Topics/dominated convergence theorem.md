## Source [^1]
- Let $f_n: \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ be a sequence of (Lebesgue) integrable functions which converge pointwise to $f$. Suppose further than $|f_n| \le g$ for all $n$ where $g$ is integrable. Then $f$ is integrable and$$ \int f \, dx = \lim_{n \to \infty} \int f_n \, dx $$
- The theorem is not true of Riemann integrals as the Dirichlet function is a pointwise limit of integrable functions which is not itself Riemann integrable.
