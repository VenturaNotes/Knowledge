## Source [^1]
- (1) (of a sequence) The cardinality of the domain of the sequence. Thus the sequence$$a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots, a_{n}$$has length $n$.
 - (2) (of a string) The number of elements in the string. 
 - (3) (of a vector) The number of elements in the vector.
## Source[^2]
- (of a line segment) The length $|AB|$ of the line segment $AB$, and the length $|\vec{AB}|$ of the directed line segment, is equal to the distance between $A$ and $B$. It is equal to zero when $A$ and $B$ coincide and is otherwise always positive.
