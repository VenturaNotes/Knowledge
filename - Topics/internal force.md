## Source [^1]
- When a system of particles or a rigid body is being considered as a whole, an internal force is a force exerted by one particle of the system on another or by one part of the rigid body on another. As far as the whole is concerned, the sum of the internal forces is zero. Compare EXTERNAL FORCE.
