## Source [^1]
- For a conservative force $\mathbf{F}$, a scalar field $\phi$ such that $\mathbf{F} = \nabla\phi$. If the domain of $\mathbf{F}$ is path-connected, then $\phi$ is unique up to a constant. If, as is conventional, gravitational potential $\phi$ is defined as gravitational potential energy per unit mass, then $\mathbf{F} = -\nabla\phi$, where $\mathbf{F}$ is the gravitational field.
