## Source [^1]
- The study of the logical basis for mathematics, and in particular attempts to establish an axiomatic basis upon which mathematics could be built. Early in the $20\text{th}$ century Bertrand Russell tried unsuccessfully to produce a unifying set of axioms for mathematics. See GÖDEL'S INCOMPLETENESS THEOREMS.
