## Source [^1]
- A matrix with exactly one column, that is, an $m \times 1$ matrix of the form$$\begin{bmatrix} a_1 \\ a_2 \\ \vdots \\ a_m \end{bmatrix}.$$
- The columns of a general matrix can be considered as individual column vectors.
