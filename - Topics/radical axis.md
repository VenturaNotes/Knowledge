## Source [^1]
- The radical axis of two circles is the line containing all points $P$ such that the lengths of the tangents from $P$ to the two circles are equal. Each figure shows a point $P$ on the radical axis and tangents touching the two circles at $T_1$ and $T_2$, with $PT_1 = PT_2$. If the circles intersect in two points, as in the second figure, the radical axis is the straight line through the two points of intersection.
