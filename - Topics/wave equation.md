## Source [^1]
- The hyperbolic partial differential equation $\partial^2 y / \partial t^2 = c^2 \partial^2 y / \partial x^2$ satisfied by small transverse vibrations of a string, where $c$ is the speed of a wave’s propagation, and first studied by d’Alembert. The general solution is $y(x,t) = f(x+ct) + g(x-ct)$, where $f$ and $g$ are arbitrary functions. The wave equation generalizes to three spatial dimensions as $\partial^2 f / \partial t^2 = c^2 \nabla^2 f$, as would be satisfied by the electric field of a light wave $E(\mathbf{x},t) = A \cos(\omega t - \mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{x} / c)$, where $\omega$ denotes frequency and $\mathbf{u}$ is a unit vector parallel to the direction of the wave.
