## Source [^1]
- To remove radicals from an expression or part of it without changing the value of the whole expression. For example, in the expression $1/(2 - \sqrt{x})$ the denominator can be rationalized by multiplying the numerator and the denominator by $2 + \sqrt{x}$ to give$$\frac{2 + \sqrt{x}}{4 - x}.$$
