## Source [^1]
- The potential energy associated with the gravitational force. When $\mathbf{F} = -\frac{GMm}{r^3}\mathbf{r}$, as in the inverse square law of gravitation, it can be shown that the gravitational potential energy $V = -GMm/r + \text{constant}$. When $\mathbf{F} = -mg\mathbf{k}$, as may be assumed near the Earth's surface, $V = mgz + \text{constant}$.
