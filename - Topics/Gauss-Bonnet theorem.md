## Source [^1]
- (for closed surfaces) The theorem states, for a smooth, orientable, closed surface $\int_X$ with Euler characteristic $\chi(X)$ that $\int_X KdA = 2\pi \chi(X)$, where $K$ is the Gaussian curvature. The result is surprising, as $K$ relates to the geometry of the surface and $\chi$ to its topology.
