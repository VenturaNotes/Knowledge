## Source [^1]
- (in 3-dimensional space) The distance from the point $P$ to the plane $p$ is the shortest distance between $P$ and a point in $p$, and is equal to $|PN|$, where $N$ is the point in $p$ such that the line $PN$ is normal to $p$. If $P$ has coordinates $(x_1, y_1, z_1)$ and $p$ has equation $ax + by + cz + d = 0$, the distance from $P$ to $p$ is equal to $$\frac{|ax_1 + by_1 + cz_1 + d|}{\sqrt{a^2 + b^2 + c^2}},$$ where $|ax_1 + by_1 + cz_1 + d|$ is the absolute value of $ax_1 + by_1 + cz_1 + d$.
