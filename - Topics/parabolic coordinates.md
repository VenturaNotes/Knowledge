## Source [^1]
- Orthogonal curvilinear coordinates $(s,t)$ in the half-plane $y > 0$ defined by$$ x = (s^2 - t^2)/2, \quad y = st \quad (s,t > 0). $$
- The curves where $s$ or $t$ are constant are parabolas. Laplace’s equation is invariant under a change to parabolic coordinates. Note that these coordinates arise from the complex, conformal map $z \mapsto z^2/2$ from the first quadrant to the upper half-plane.
