## Source [^1]
- (Hall’s Marriage Theorem) Gives conditions for when a part of a bipartite graph has a matching. For a bipartite graph $G$ with parts $A$ and $B$, there is a matching for every element of $A$ if and only if for every $W \subseteq A$ it is the case that $|W| \leq |N_G(W)|$, where $N_G(W)$ denotes the neighborhood of $W$.
