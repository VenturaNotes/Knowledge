## Source [^1]
- A sequence $\{x_n\}$ which converges to a limit $l$ is said to have rate (or order) of convergence $r \le 1$ if there exists $M$ such that$$|x_{n+1} - l| \le M|x_n - l|^r$$for sufficiently large $n$. For $r = 1$, it is further required that $M \le 1$. If $r = 2$, then the convergence is said to be quadratic. If $r = 1$ and$$\lim_{n \to \infty} \frac{|x_{n+1} - l|}{|x_n - l|} = m,$$then the convergence is said to be superlinear, linear, or sublinear depending on whether $m = 0, 0 < m < 1$ or $m = 1$.
