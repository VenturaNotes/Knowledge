## Source [^1]
- For $\mathbf{A}$, a square matrix $[a_{ij}]$, the matrix of cofactors is the matrix, of the same order, that is obtained by replacing each entry $a_{ij}$ by its cofactor $A_{ij}$. It is used to find the adjoint of $\mathbf{A}$ and hence the inverse $\mathbf{A}^{-1}$.
