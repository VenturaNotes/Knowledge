## Source [^1]
- Let $V$ be a vector space and $v_1, \dots, v_m, w_1, \dots, w_n \in V$. If the $v_i$ are linearly independent and the $w_i$ span $V$, then, with some possible reordering of the $w_i$,$$ \{v_1, \dots, v_m, w_{m+1}, \dots, w_n\} $$spans $V$. Thus, each $v_i$ can be introduced one at a time in exchange for some $w_j$ and consequentially $m \le n$. This implies all bases of $V$ have the same cardinality and that the dimension of $V$ is well defined.
