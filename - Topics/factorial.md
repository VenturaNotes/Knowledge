## Source [^1]
- For a positive integer $n$, the notation $n!$ (read as 'n factorial' or 'n shriek') is used for the product $n(n-1)(n-2)\dots \times 2 \times 1$. Thus $4! = 4 \times 3 \times 2 \times 1 = 24$. Also, by definition, $0! = 1$. See also GAMMA FUNCTION.
- For a positive integer $n$, the 'double factorial' notation $n!!$ denotes the products$$\begin{align}&n \times (n-2) \times (n-4) \times \dots \times 1 = \frac{(2k)!}{2^k k!} \quad \\&\text{when} \quad n = 2k-1;\\&n \times (n-2) \times (n-4) \times \dots \times 2 = 2^k k! \quad \text{when} \quad n = 2k.\end{align}$$
