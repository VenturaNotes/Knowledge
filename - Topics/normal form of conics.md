## Source [^1]
- The ellipse, parabola, and hyperbola have the following normal forms:
	- ellipse (and circle): $\frac{x^2}{a^2} + \frac{y^2}{b^2} = 1$ where $0 < b \leq a$.
	- parabola: $y^2 = 4ax$ where $0 < a$.
	- hyperbola: $\frac{x^2}{a^2} - \frac{y^2}{b^2} = 1$ where $0 < a, b$.
- By means of a rotation and translation, any non-degenerate conic can be transformed into one of the above forms, and no two distinct normal forms are congruent. See also QUADRICS.
