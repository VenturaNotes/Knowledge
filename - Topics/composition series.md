## Source [^1]
- Subgroups $G_0, G_1, \dots, G_n$ of a finite group $G$ such that (i) $\{e\} = G_0$ and $G = G_n$, (ii) $G_k$ is a normal subgroup of $G_{k+1}$ for $0 \le k < n$, (iii) the quotient groups $G_{k+1}/G_k$, called composition factors, are all simple. $\{e\} \rhd A_3 \rhd S_3$ is a composition series for $S_3$ as $A_3/\{e\} \cong C_3$ and $S_3/A_3 \cong C_2$ are simple. See JORDAN-HÖLDER THEOREM.
