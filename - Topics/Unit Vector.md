## Source [^1]
- Unit Vector Formula
	- $\frac {\vec v}{||\vec v||}$
## Source[^2]
- A vector whose magnitude is exactly one, but whose orientation is arbitrary.
## Source[^3]
- A vector with magnitude, or length, equal to 1. For any non-zero vector $\mathbf{a}$, a unit vector in the direction of $\mathbf{a}$ is $\mathbf{a}/|\mathbf{a}|$.
