## Source [^1]
- Abbreviation for ‘with respect to’. In particular to identify the variable in an integration or differentiation process so $f'(x) = \frac{dy}{dx}$ is the differential of $f(x)$ wrt $x$, and $\int \pi(x^2 + k) dx$ is an integral wrt $x$.
