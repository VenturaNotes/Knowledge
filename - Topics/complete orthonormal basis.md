## Source [^1]
- In a Hilbert space $X$, an orthonormal sequence $(x_n)$ which is a Schauder basis. This is equivalent to Parseval’s identity holding for all $x \in X$, or to the only solution of $\langle x, x_n \rangle = 0$, for all $n$, being $x = 0$.
