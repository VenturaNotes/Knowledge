## Source [^1]
- (1847–1921) Russian mathematician and engineer, often labelled ‘the father of Russian aviation’, he was a pioneer in the early study of aerodynamics, hydrodynamics, and flight. He was the first to mathematically model aerodynamic lift. See JOUKOVSKI MAP.
