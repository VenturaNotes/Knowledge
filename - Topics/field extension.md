## Source [^1]
- A pair of fields $F$ and $K$, where the base field $F$ is a subfield of $K$. Importantly, $K$ is a vector space over $F$, and the degree of the extension is defined as the dimension of $K$ as such a vector space. For example, $\mathbb{C}$ is a degree 2 extension of $\mathbb{R}$ as $\{1, i\}$ is a basis. See SIMPLE EXTENSION, TOWER LAW.
