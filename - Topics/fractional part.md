## Source [^1]
- (1) The part of a number to the right of the radix point. 
- (2) Another name for mantissa or significand. See FLOATING-POINT NOTATION.
## Source[^2]
- For any real number $x$, its fractional part is equal to $x - [x]$, where $[x]$ is the integer part of $x$. It may be denoted by $\{x\}$. The fractional part $r$ of any real number always satisfies $0 \le r < 1$.
