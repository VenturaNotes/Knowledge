## Source [^1]
- (ring) If $I$ is an ideal of a ring $R$, then the coset of an element $x$ of $R$ is $$x + I = \{x + i : i \in I\}$$
- See quotient ring
---
- (group) If H is a subgroup of a group G, then for any element, $x$ of $G$ there is a left coset $xH$ consisting of all the elements $xh$, where $h$ is an element of $H$. Similarly, there is a right coset, $Hx$, with elements $hx$. See also Lagrange's Theorem, normal subgroup, quotient group.
