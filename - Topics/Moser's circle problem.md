## Source [^1]
- Named after Leo Moser, this result is commonly cited to highlight the need to prove that perceived patterns do indeed continue forever. The maximum number of regions that the chords between $n$ points on a circle divide the interior into is $1, 2, 4, 8, 16$ for $n = 1, 2, 3, 4, 5$. (In the figure $n = 4$.)
- ![[Pasted image 20260909205459.png|303]]
	- 4 points and 8 regions
- It would seem reasonable to suppose the pattern continues as $2^{n-1}$, but in fact the next term is 31, and the $n$th term of the sequence is$$ 1 + \binom{n}{2} + \binom{n}{4}. $$
