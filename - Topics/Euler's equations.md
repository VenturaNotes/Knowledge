## Source [^1]
- (fluid dynamics) Euler's equations for the flow of an inviscid, incompressible fluid with flow velocity $\mathbf{u}$ state$$\text{div}\mathbf{u} = 0, \quad \frac{D\mathbf{u}}{Dt} = -\frac{1}{\rho}\nabla p + \mathbf{g}.$$Here $\rho$ denotes the (constant) density, $p$ denotes pressure, $\mathbf{g}$ denotes acceleration due to gravity, and $D/Dt$ is the convective derivative. Compare NAVIER-STOKES EQUATIONS.
