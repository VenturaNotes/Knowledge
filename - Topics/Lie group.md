## Source [^1]
- A Lie group $G$ is a group $G$ that also has the structure of a smooth manifold. Further, the group operations of multiplication $G \times G \to G$ and inversion $G \to G$ must be smooth. An example is $S^1$ considered as unit modulus complex numbers under multiplication. Real and complex matrix groups are further examples. The Lie correspondence connects Lie groups and Lie algebras, and there is a rich representation theory of compact Lie groups largely due to Weyl. See also TOPOLOGICAL GROUP.
