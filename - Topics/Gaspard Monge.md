## Source [^1]
- (1746–1818) French mathematician who was a high-ranking minister in Napoleon’s government and influential in the founding of the École Polytechnique in Paris. The courses in geometry that he taught there were concerned to a great extent with the subjects of his research: he was the originator of what is called descriptive geometry and the founder of modern analytical solid geometry.
