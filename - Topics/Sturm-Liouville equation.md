## Source [^1]
- A second-order differential equation$$\frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}x}\left[p(x)\frac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x}\right] + [\lambda\omega(x) - q(x)]y=0$$where $\omega(x)$ is called the 'weighting function'. The values of $\lambda$ for which there is a solution are the eigenvalue and the corresponding solutions are the eigenfunctions.
