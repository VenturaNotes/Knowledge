## Source [^1]
- The following paradox: let $n$ be the largest integer; if $n > 1$, then $n^2 > n$, which contradicts $n$ being the largest integer and so $n = 1$ is the largest integer. The ridiculous conclusion shows the danger of simply assuming a solution to an optimization problem exists.
