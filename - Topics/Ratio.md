## Source [^1]
- The quotient of two numbers or quantities giving their relative size. The ratio of $x$ to $y$ is written as $x : y$ and is unchanged if both quantities are multiplied or divided by the same quantity. So $2 : 3$ is the same as $6 : 9$ and $1 : \frac{3}{2}$. Where a ratio is expressed in the form $1 : a$ it is called a unitary ratio, and this form makes comparison of ratios much easier.
