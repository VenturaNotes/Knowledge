## Source [^1]
- (1596–1650) French philosopher and mathematician who in mathematics is known mainly for his methods of applying algebra to geometry, from which analytic geometry developed via the use of Cartesian coordinates, the nascent ideas of which date back to his La Géométrie of 1637.
