## Source [^1]
- For an $m \times n$ matrix $\mathbf{M}$ of rank $r$, there exists an orthogonal $m \times m$ matrix $\mathbf{P}$ and an orthogonal $n \times n$ matrix $\mathbf{Q}$ such that$$ \mathbf{PMQ} = \begin{bmatrix} \mathbf{D} & \mathbf{0} \\ \mathbf{0} & \mathbf{0} \end{bmatrix}, $$where $\mathbf{D}$ is an $r \times r$ diagonal matrix with decreasing positive entries. The pseudoinverse $\mathbf{M}^+$ of $\mathbf{M}$ is given by$$ \mathbf{M}^+ = \mathbf{Q} \begin{bmatrix} \mathbf{D}^{-1} & \mathbf{0} \\ \mathbf{0} & \mathbf{0} \end{bmatrix} \mathbf{P}. $$
