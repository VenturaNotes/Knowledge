## Source [^1]
- (1965-) British mathematician who has done much to improve the communication of mathematical ideas through regular newspaper articles, the Royal Institution Christmas Lectures in 2006, and books. He was awarded the Berwick Prize of the London Mathematical Society in 2001 for his work on zeta functions.
