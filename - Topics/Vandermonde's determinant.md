## Source [^1]
- For $n$ real numbers $x_1, x_2, \dots, x_n$, the determinant$$\begin{vmatrix} 1 & x_1 & x_1^2 & \dots & x_1^{n-1} \\ 1 & x_2 & x_2^2 & \dots & x_2^{n-1} \\ 1 & x_3 & x_3^2 & \dots & x_3^{n-1} \\ \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & \ddots & \vdots \\ 1 & x_n & x_n^2 & \dots & x_n^{n-1} \end{vmatrix} = \prod_{i>j} (x_i - x_j)$$
- Note that the determinant is non-zero, and so the matrix is invertible, if and only if the $x_i$ are distinct.
