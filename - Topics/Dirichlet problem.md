## Source [^1]
- Originally the Dirichlet problem involved solving Laplace's equation inside a region and meeting specified values on the boundary. Now the term might relate to any PDE. That the solution needs to meet specified values on the boundary is called a Dirichlet boundary condition. Compare NEUMANN CONDITION, ROBIN BOUNDARY CONDITION, POISSON'S INTEGRAL FORMULA.
