## Source [^1]
- If $A$ and $B$ are two points on a line, the part of the line between and including $A$ and $B$ is a line segment. This may be denoted by $AB$ or $BA$. The length of the line segment is the distance between $A$ and $B$. Compare DIRECTED LINE SEGMENT.
