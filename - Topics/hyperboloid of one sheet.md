- The axial planes are planes of symmetry. The section by a plane $z = k$ parallel to the $xy$-plane is an ellipse (a circle if $a = b$). The hyperboloid is all in one piece or sheet. Sections by planes parallel to the other two axial planes are hyperbolas.
