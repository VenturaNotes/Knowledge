## Source [^1]
- A ring is Noetherian if any ascending chain of ideals $I_1 \subseteq I_2 \subseteq \dots \subseteq I_k \subseteq \dots$ eventually becomes constant. The condition guarantees finite decomposition in different senses, for example decomposing varieties into finite irreducible components or, in $\mathbb{Z}$, to factorization of integers into primes. Principal ideal domains are Noetherian. See HILBERT'S BASIS THEOREM.
