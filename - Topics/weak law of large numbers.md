## Source [^1]
- Given a random variable $X$ with finite variance, the sample average of the first $n$ observations; that is, $m_n = \frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^n x_i$ converges in probability to the mean $\mu = E(X)$. Compare STRONG LAW OF LARGE NUMBERS.
