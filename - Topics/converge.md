## Source [^1]
- (series) The infinite series $a_1 + a_2 + a_3 + \dots$ is said to converge if its partial sums converge as a sequence; that is, there exists a limit $L$ such that for all $n > N, |\sum_{i=1}^n a_i - L| < \epsilon$. Note that for a series to converge the sequence made up of its terms must converge to 0, though the converse is not true as demonstrated by the harmonic series.
