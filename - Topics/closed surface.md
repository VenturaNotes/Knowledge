## Source [^1]
- A surface which is compact has no boundary or bounding curve. Examples include the sphere, torus, and Klein bottle but not a polygon or Möbius strip. More generally a closed manifold is compact and without boundary. See CLASSIFICATION THEOREM FOR SURFACES.
