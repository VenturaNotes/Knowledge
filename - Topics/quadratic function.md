## Source [^1]
- (quadratic polynomial) A quadratic function is a real function $f$ such that $f(x) = ax^2 + bx + c$ for all $x$ in $\mathbb{R}$, where $a, b$ and $c$ are real numbers, with $a \neq 0$. The graph $y = f(x)$ of such a function is a parabola with its axis parallel to the $y$-axis, and with its vertex downwards if $a > 0$ and upwards if $a < 0$. The term equally applies to degree two polynomials over other fields and rings.
