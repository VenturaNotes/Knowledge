## Source [^1]
- $n$. A statistical procedure that is closely related to analysis of variance (ANOVA) and that determines the significance of differences among several group means, but unlike the standard (univariate) analysis of variance analyses more than one dependent variable simultaneously. MANOVA abbrev.
