## Source [^1]
- An ideal $I$ in a ring $R$ is said to be prime if whenever $ab \in I$, then $a \in I$ or $b \in I$. The ideal $I$ is prime if and only if the quotient ring $R/I$ is an integral domain. Maximal ideals are prime.
