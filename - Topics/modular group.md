## Source [^1]
- The group of Möbius transformations $z \mapsto (az + b)/(cz + d)$ under composition, where $a, b, c, d$ are integers and $ad - bc = 1$. The group is generated by the maps $z \mapsto z + 1$ and $z \mapsto -1/z$ and acts on the upper half plane $\text{Im } z > 0$. The modular group can identified with the group $\text{SL}(2, \mathbb{Z})$ of $2 \times 2$ matrices with integer entries and determinant 1, provided a matrix is considered equivalent to its negative.
