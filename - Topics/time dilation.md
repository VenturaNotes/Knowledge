## Source [^1]
- In special relativity, if an observer $O'$ is travelling at speed $v$ relative to an observer $O$, then two events on the timeline of $O'$ which are separated by time $T$ (as measure by $O'$) will be measured by $O$ as being $T/\sqrt{1 - v^2/c^2}$ apart. This is greater than $T$. Here $c$ denotes the speed of light, so the difference will be small unless $v$ is comparable with $c$. Somewhat counterintuitively, the same time dilation applies when $O'$ measures events on the timeline of $O$.
