## Source [^1]
- Let $L, K, F$ be fields with $F \subseteq K \subseteq L$. If $\{k_i\}$ is a basis for $K$ over $F$, and $\{l_j\}$ is a basis for $L$ over $K$, then $\{k_i l_j\}$ is a basis for $L$ over $F$. Hence, the degree of $L$ over $F$ equals the product of the degree of $L$ over $K$ with the degree of $K$ over $F$.
