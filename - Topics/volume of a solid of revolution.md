## Source [^1]
- Let $y=f(x)$ be the graph of a function $f$, a continuous function on $[a,b]$, and such that $f(x) \geq 0$ for all $x$ in $[a,b]$. The volume $V$ of the solid of revolution obtained by rotating, through one revolution about the $x$-axis, the region bounded by the curve $y=f(x)$, the $x$-axis and the lines $x=a$ and $x=b$, is given by$$V = \int_a^b \pi y^2 dx = \int_a^b \pi (f(x))^2 dx.$$
### Parametric form
- For the curve $x = x(t)$, $y = y(t)$ ($t \in [\alpha, \beta]$), the volume $V$ is given by$$V = \int_\alpha^\beta \pi y^2 \frac{dx}{dt} dt = \int_\alpha^\beta \pi (y(t))^2 x'(t) dt.$$
