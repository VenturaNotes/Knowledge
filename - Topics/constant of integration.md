## Source [^1]
- If $\varphi$ is a particular antiderivative of a continuous function $f$, then any antiderivative of $f$ differs from $\varphi$ by a constant. It is common, therefore, to write$\int f(x) dx = \varphi(x) + c$ where $c$, an arbitrary constant, is the constant of integration.
