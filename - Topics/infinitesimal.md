## Source [^1]
- A variable whose limit is zero, usually involving increments of functions, and of particular use in defining differentiation. The ratio $\frac{\delta y}{\delta x}$ tends to the differential $\frac{dy}{dx}$ as $\delta x \to 0$. See also HYPERREALS.
