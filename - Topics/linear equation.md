## Source[^1]
- An equation that can be written as $a_1x_1 + a_2x_2 + a_3x_3 + ... + a_nx_n = b$ where $a_1,a_2,...a_n, b$ are real or complex numbers
	- Basically a standard form equation where we have variables and coefficients equal to some value
## Source[^2]
- Consider, in turn, linear equations in one, two, and three variables. A linear equation in one variable $x$ is an equation of the form $ax + b = 0$. If $a \neq 0$, this has the solution $x = -b/a$. A linear equation in two variables $x$ and $y$ is an equation of the form $ax + by + c = 0$. If $a$ and $b$ are not both zero, and $x$ and $y$ are taken as Cartesian coordinates in the plane, the equation is that of a straight line. A linear equation in three variables $x, y,$ and $z$ has the form $ax + by + cz + d = 0$. If $a, b,$ and $c$ are not all zero, and $x, y,$ and $z$ are taken as Cartesian coordinates in 3-dimensional space, the equation is that of a plane. See also SIMULTANEOUS LINEAR EQUATIONS.
