## Source [^1]
- A function, $f$, on a metric space, $X$, which decreases the distance, $d$, between any two points in $X$ by at least some given factor, i.e. there exists $a < 1, d(f(x), f(y)) \leq a \times d(x,y)$ for all $x, y \in X$.
