## Source [^1]
- The generalization of the geometric distribution to the waiting time for the $k$th success in a sequence of independent experiments, all with the same probabilities of success. The probability mass function is given by$$\text{Pr}\{X=r\} = \binom{r-1}{k-1}p^k(1-p)^{r-k}.$$
- This is because the $k$th success will be on the $r$th trial if the $r$th trial is a success and there are $k-1$ successful trials in the previous $r-1$ trials. See APPENDIX 15.
