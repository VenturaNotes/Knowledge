## Source [^1]
- A prime of the form $2^p - 1$, where $p$ is a prime. The number of known primes of this form is over 50 and keeps increasing as they are discovered by using computers. In 2020, the largest known was $2^{82589933} - 1$. Each Mersenne prime gives rise to an even perfect number. It is an open problem whether there are infinitely many Mersenne primes.
