## Source [^1]
- A graph that can be drawn on paper (with points representing vertices and lines joining vertices representing edges) in such a way that edges intersect only at vertices.
## Source[^2]
- A graph that can be drawn in a plane in such a way that no two edges cross. The complete graph $K_4$, for example, is planar as either drawing of it in the figure shows. Neither the complete graph $K_5$ nor the complete bipartite graph $K_{3,3}$ is planar. See DUAL GRAPH, EULER’S THEOREM, KURATOWSKI’S THEOREM.
