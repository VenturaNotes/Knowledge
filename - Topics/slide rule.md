## Source [^1]
- A mechanical device used for mathematical calculations such as multiplication and division. In the simplest form, one piece slides alongside another piece, each piece being marked with a logarithmic scale. To multiply $x$ and $y$, the reading ‘$x$’ on one scale is placed opposite the ‘$l$’ on the other scale, and the required product ‘$xy$’ then appears opposite the ‘$y$’. Slide rules have now been superseded by calculators and computers.
