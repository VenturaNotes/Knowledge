## Source [^1]
- An error which is not random and is therefore liable to introduce bias into an estimator. For example,$$ \sum_{r=1}^{n} \frac{(x_i - \bar{x})^2}{n} $$has a tendency to underestimate the population variance. In a production process, if one of ten prongs on a rotating drum is broken then every tenth item on the production line will be faulty in addition to any random faults which may occur.
