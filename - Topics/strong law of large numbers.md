## Source [^1]
- Given a random variable $X$ with finite variance, the sample mean of the first $n$ observations, that is,$$ \frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^n X_i $$converges almost surely to the mean $\mu = E(X)$. Compare WEAK LAW OF LARGE NUMBERS.
