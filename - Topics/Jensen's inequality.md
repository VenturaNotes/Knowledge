## Source [^1]
- The inequality$$f(tx_1 + (1-t)x_2) \leq tf(x_1) + (1-t)f(x_2)$$or a convex (= concave up) function $f$ and $0 \leq t \leq 1$. This implies that the chord connecting the points $(x_1, f(x_1))$ and $(x_2, f(x_2))$ lies above the graph $y = f(x)$ for the interval $x_1 \leq x \leq x_2$. In probability, the inequality is commonly expressed as $f(\text{E}(X)) \leq \text{E}(f(X))$ where $X$ is a random variable.
