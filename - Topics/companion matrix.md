## Source [^1]
- For a monic polynomial $f(x) = x^n + a_{n-1}x^{n-1} + \dots + a_1x + a_0$, the companion matrix of $f$ is the $n \times n$ matrix$$ \begin{bmatrix} 0 & 0 & \dots & \dots & 0 & -a_0 \\ 1 & 0 & 0 & \dots & 0 & -a_1 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 & \dots & 0 & -a_2 \\ \vdots & 0 & 1 & \ddots & \vdots & \vdots \\ \vdots & \vdots & \ddots & \ddots & 0 & -a_{n-2} \\ 0 & 0 & \dots & 0 & 1 & -a_{n-1} \end{bmatrix} $$
- Its characteristic polynomial and minimal polynomial both equal $f(x)$. See RATIONAL CANONICAL FORM.
