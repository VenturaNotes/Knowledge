## Source [^1]
- (1) In economics, a model of interaction between agents through the market or through social institutions in which the joint productivity of matched agents or, more generally, the pay-offs, depend on the individual characteristics of both sides. Matching models are used, for example, in empirical studies of the labor market.
- (2) See PROPENSITY SCORE MATCHING.
## Source[^2]
- In a graph, a matching is a set of edges with no vertices in common. See also HALL’S THEOREM.
