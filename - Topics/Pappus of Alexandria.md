## Source [^1]
- (about AD 320) Considered to be the last great ancient Greek geometer. He wrote commentaries on Euclid and Ptolemy, but most valuable is his Synagoge (‘Collection’), which contains detailed accounts of much Greek mathematics, some of which would otherwise be unknown.
