## Source [^1]
- A representation $\rho : G \to \operatorname{GL}(V)$ of a group $G$ is irreducible if the only invariant subspaces of $V$ are $\{0\}$ and $V$. A subspace $W$ of $V$ is invariant in this sense if $\rho(g)w \in W$ for all $w \in W$ and $g \in G$.
