## Source [^1]
- For an inviscid, irrotational, incompressible flow $u(z)$ in the complex plane, with velocity potential $\phi$ and stream function $\psi$, the complex potential is $w = \phi + i\psi$. Then $u(z) = \overline{dw/dz}$ where the bar denotes conjugate.
