## Source [^1]
- A general term relating to a space of functions between two given sets $X$ and $Y$. If the codomain has further structure (e.g. if $Y = \mathbb{R}$), then functions can be pointwise added and multiplied, forming an algebra, and given a partial order. If the domain $X$ is a topological space, then the space $C^*(X)$ of bounded, continuous functions real-valued functions on $X$ can be made into a normed vector space with $\|f\| = \sup_{x \in X} |f(x)|$. For vector spaces $V$ and $W$, then $\text{Hom}(V, W)$, denoting the space of linear maps from $V$ to $W$, is a vector space itself. See also $C^0, C^1, C^\infty, C^\omega, L^p$.
