## Source [^1]
- Let $P$ be a point on a smooth surface. The tangent plane at $P$ is the plane through $P$ that touches the surface. Equally, it is the plane containing all the tangent lines to smooth curves in the surface that pass through $P$. If $\mathbf{r}(u, v)$ is a parameterization of the surface about $P = \mathbf{r}(u_0, v_0)$, then the tangent plane is the plane through $P$ parallel to $\partial \mathbf{r}/\partial u(u_0, v_0)$ and $\partial \mathbf{r}/\partial v(u_0, v_0)$.
