## Source [^1]
- In rounding or truncation of a number to $n$ decimal places, the original is replaced by a number with just $n$ digits after the decimal point. When the rounding takes place to the left of the decimal point, a phrase such as 'to the nearest 10' or 'to the nearest 1000' is used. To say that $a = 1.9$ to 1 decimal place means that the exact value of $a$ becomes 1.9 after rounding to 1 decimal place, and so $1.85 \leq a < 1.95$. 'Decimal places' is often abbreviated to 'dp'.
