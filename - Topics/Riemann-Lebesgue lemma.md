## Source [^1]
- Let $f : \mathbb{R} \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ be Lebesgue integrable (see LEBESGUE INTEGRATION). Then the integrals$$\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} f(x) \cos nx \, \text{dx} \quad \text{and} \quad \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} f(x) \sin nx \, \text{dx}$$exist and converge to $0$ and $n \rightarrow \infty$. See FOURIER COEFFICIENTS.
