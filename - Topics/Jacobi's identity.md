## Source [^1]
- For a set with addition and product $(X, Y) \mapsto [X, Y]$ defined, the identity $[X, [Y, Z]] + [Y, [Z, X]] + [Z, [X, Y]] = 0$. The identity is satisfied by the vector sum and vector product, by the sum and commutator product $[X, Y] = XY - YX$ for $n \times n$ matrices, and in Lie algebras.
