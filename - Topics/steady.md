## Source [^1]
- (steady state) The term steady in general relates to a scenario that is unchanging with time. In fluid mechanics, a flow is steady if it is unvarying with time. A ‘steady state’ solution to a PDE involving spatial and temporal variables is one which is independent of time; on occasion, this is naturally the solution that the system converges to as time progresses.
