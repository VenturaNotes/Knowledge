## Source [^1]
- Given a square matrix $\mathbf{A}$ with an eigenvalue $\lambda$, the eigenspace of $\lambda$ is the kernel of $\mathbf{A} - \lambda\mathbf{I}$, that is the subspace of vectors $\mathbf{x}$ satisfying $\mathbf{Ax} = \lambda\mathbf{x}$. The eigenspaces form a direct sum, and $\mathbf{A}$ is diagonalizable if and only if this direct sum equals the whole space. The definition applies equally to a linear map of a vector space.
