## Source [^1]
- A complex matrix $M$ such that $M = M^*$ where $M^* = \overline{M}^T$ is the conjugate transpose of $M$. Hermitian matrices are the complex equivalent of real symmetric matrices, to which a version of the spectral theorem applies. Hermitian linear maps are the self-adjoint linear maps of complex inner product spaces. In quantum theory, observables are represented by Hermitian maps and matrices.
