## Source [^1]
- A great circle on the Earth, assumed to be a sphere, through the north and south poles or such a semi-circle between poles. More generally, a meridian on a surface of revolution is a copy of the generating curve that has been rotated through some fixed angle. The meridians are then normal to the latitudes.
