## Source [^1]
- A group formed by the cosets of a normal subgroup. Let $G/H$ denote the set of cosets of a subgroup $H$ of a group $G$. The group operation on $G$ induces a well-defined group operation on $G/H$ given by $(g_1H)(g_2H) = (g_1g_2)H$ if and only if $H$ is a normal subgroup of $G$, By Lagrange's theorem, if $G$ is finite then $G/H$ has order $|G|/|H|$.
