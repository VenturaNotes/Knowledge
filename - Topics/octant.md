## Source [^1]
- In a 3-dimensional Cartesian coordinate system, the axial planes divide the rest of the space into eight regions called octants. The set of points $\{(x, y, z) \mid x, y, z > 0\}$ may be called the positive (or first) octant. Compare QUADRANT.
