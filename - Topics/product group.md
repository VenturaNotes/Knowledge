## Source [^1]
- Given two groups $(G, \cdot)$ and $(H, \circ)$, the product group is the Cartesian set $G \times H$ together with the group operation $*$ given by$$(g_1, h_1) * (g_2, h_2) = (g_1 \cdot g_2, h_1 \circ h_2).$$
- The product $G \times H$ is abelian if and only if $G$ and $H$ are both abelian. $G \times H$ is also called the direct product. See also SEMI-DIRECT PRODUCT.
