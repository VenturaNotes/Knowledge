## Source [^1]
- Given a set $A$ and $B \subseteq A$, then inclusion is the map $\iota: B \to A$ defined by $\iota(b) = b$ for all $b$ in $B$. Note that $\iota$ is the restriction of the identity map on $A$ to $B$.
