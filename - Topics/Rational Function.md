## Source [^1]
- A rational function is one $f$ of the form $f(x) = g(x)/h(x)$, where $g(x)$ and $h(x)$ are polynomials, usually with coefficients from a field $F$. The rational functions themselves then make a field denoted $F(x)$. Addition and multiplication are defined as expected by$$\begin{align}&\frac{f_1(x)}{g_1(x)} + \frac{f_2(x)}{g_2(x)} = \frac{g_2(x)f_1(x) + f_2(x)g_1(x)}{g_1(x)g_2(x)},\\&\left(\frac{f_1(x)}{g_1(x)}\right)\left(\frac{f_2(x)}{g_2(x)}\right) = \frac{f_1(x)f_2(x)}{g_1(x)g_2(x)}.\end{align}$$
- Compare POLYNOMIAL RING.
