## Source [^1]
- (positive semidefinite) A symmetric matrix $\mathbf{A}$ is positive definite if $\mathbf{x}^{\text{T}}\mathbf{Ax} > 0$ for all non-zero column vectors $\mathbf{x}$ and is positive semidefinite if $\mathbf{x}^{\text{T}}\mathbf{Ax} \ge 0$ for all $\mathbf{x}$. A symmetric matrix is positive definite (resp. semidefinite) if and only if its eigenvalues are positive (resp. non-negative). See CHOLESKY'S DECOMPOSITION.
