## Source[^2]
- The trace of a square matrix is the sum of the entries in the main diagonal. It satisfies $\text{trace}(\mathbf{AB}) = \text{trace}(\mathbf{BA})$, where $\mathbf{A}$ is $m \times n$ and $\mathbf{B}$ is $n \times m$. For an $n \times n$ matrix $\mathbf{A}$, then $\text{trace}(\mathbf{A})$ is the coefficient of $x^{n-1}$ in the characteristic polynomial of $\mathbf{A}$.
