## Source [^1]
- A differential equation of the form$$\frac{dy}{dx} + P(x)y = Q(x)$$where $P$ and $Q$ are given functions. One method of solution is to multiply both sides of the equation by an integrating factor $\mu(x)$, given by$$\mu(x) = \exp\left(\int P(x) \, dx\right)$$
- This choice is made because then the left-hand side of the equation becomes the derivative of $\mu(x)y$, and the solution can be found by integration.
- In the case where $Q(x) = 0$, this can also be treated as a separable-variable first-order differential equation.
