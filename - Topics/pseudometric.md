## Source [^1]
- One of the conditions for a distance measure $d$ to be a metric is that $d(x,y) = 0$ implies that $x=y$. If this condition is relaxed so that the distance between two different points can be zero, then $d$ is said to be a pseudometric, and the space is said to be a pseudometric space.
