## Source [^1]
- The principle of propositional logic that every statement is either true or false. Equivalently, this means for any statement $P$ then $P \lor (\neg P)$ is true. Some philosophies of mathematics, such as intuitionism, reject this principle and proof by contradiction, on the grounds that $\neg P$ being provably false is not the same as a proof of $P$.
