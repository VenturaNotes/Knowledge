## Source [^1]
- A dynamic influence that causes a particle or body to accelerate. (See NEWTON'S LAWS OF MOTION.) Commonly experienced forces are gravity, contact forces (such as friction and air resistance), forces within a body that deform or restore its shape, and electrical and magnetic forces.
- In general, force is a vector quantity with a magnitude, a direction, and also a point of application. (See MOMENT (IN MECHANICS).) Force has the dimensions $MLT^{-2}$, and the SI unit of measurement is the newton.
