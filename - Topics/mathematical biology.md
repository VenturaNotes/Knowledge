## Source [^1]
- The area of mathematics modelling biological systems, including such models as SIR epidemiology models, population growth as with the logistic map, and predator–prey models such as the Lotka-Volterra equations. The increase in large data sets and computing power has meant much more complex biological systems can now be modeled.
