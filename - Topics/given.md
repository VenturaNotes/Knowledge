## Source [^1]
- Something which is already known independently, or something which is to be used in the course of a proof. For example the epsilon-delta method of proof usually states 'Given $\varepsilon > 0$, there exists a $\delta$', by which is meant 'For any $\varepsilon$ you choose, no matter how small, I can find a value of $\delta$ for which...'.
