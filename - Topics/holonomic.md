## Source [^1]
- In a mechanical system, with generalized coordinates $q_1, q_2, \dots, q_n$, a holonomic constraint is one involving only $q_1, q_2, \dots, q_n$ and time $t$. For example, such a constraint would be $z = r^2$ for a particle at $(r, \theta, z)$ moving on the surface of a paraboloid. See LAGRANGE’S EQUATIONS.
