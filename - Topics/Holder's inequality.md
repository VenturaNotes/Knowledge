## Source [^1]
- Hölder’s inequality on $\mathbb{R}^n$ states that$$|\mathbf{x} \cdot \mathbf{y}| \le \|\mathbf{x}\|_p \|\mathbf{y}\|_q$$where $p, q \ge 1$ and $1/p + 1/q = 1$ (see P-NORMS). When $p = q = 2$, this gives the Cauchy-Schwarz inequality.
