## Source [^1]
- An informal name for an accommodation bill. Kite-flying or kiting is the discounting of a kite (accommodation bill) at a bank, knowing that the person on whom it is drawn will dishonor it.
## Source[^2]
- A quadrilateral that has two adjacent sides of equal length and the other two sides of equal length. If the kite $ABCD$ has $AB = AD$ and $CB = CD$, the diagonals $AC$ and $BD$ are perpendicular, and $AC$ bisects $BD$.
