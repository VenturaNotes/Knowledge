## Source [^1]
- A test for convergence or divergence of an infinite series $\sum a_n$, due to D'Alembert. If the ratio$$\left| \frac{a_{n+1}}{a_n} \right| \to k \text{ as } n \to \infty$$then when $k < 1$ the series converges absolutely, and for $k > 1$ the series diverges. If $k = 1$, the ratio test is not sufficient to determine whether it converges. For example, $k = 1$ for both the series $\sum 1/n$ and $\sum 1/n^2$, but the first series diverges whereas the second converges.
