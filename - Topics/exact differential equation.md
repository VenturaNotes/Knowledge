## Source [^1]
- A differential equation $P dx + Q dy = 0$ is exact if $P dx + Q dy$ is the total differential of a function $f(x,y)$ so that the general solution has the from $f = \text{constant}$. Even when $P dx + Q dy$ is not an exact differential, an integrating factor $m(x,y)$ may be found so that $m P dx + m Q dy$ is exact.
