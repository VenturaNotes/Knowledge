## Source [^1]
- ($1984\text{-}$) Mathematician and popularizer of mathematics. She has a long-running radio programme with the geneticist Adam Rutherford and has written several books. Her work often relates to what mathematics and statistics can say about societal behavior. In $2019$ she gave the Royal Institution Christmas lectures, and in $2020$ she received the LMS Zeeman medal for her public engagement on mathematics.
