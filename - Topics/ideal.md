## Source [^1]
- A subring $I$ of a ring $R$ such that for every $a$ in $R$ and every $x$ in $I$ both $ax$ and $xa$ are in $I$. Every ideal of $\mathbb{Z}$ is of the form $n\mathbb{Z}$ for some $n$ and so principal. Ideals are those subrings for which a well-defined quotient ring $R/I$ can be defined. See MAXIMAL IDEAL, PRIME IDEAL.
