## Source [^1]
- A plane with respect to which a 3-dimensional figure has reflective symmetry. So for a cylinder, any plane containing parallel diameters in the two circular faces of the cylinder will be a plane of symmetry, as will the plane parallel to the two circular ends and equidistant from them.
