## Source [^1]
- A complex number $z = x + \mathrm{i}y$ written in the form$$r (\sin \phi + \mathrm {i} \cos \phi)$$where $r$ is the modulus and $\phi$ is the argument of that number.
## Source[^2]
- For a nonzero complex number $z$, let $r = |z|$ and $\theta = \arg z$. Then $z = r(\cos \theta + i \sin \theta)$, and this is the polar form of $z$. It may also be written $z = re^{i\theta}$ using Euler's formula.
