## Source [^1]
- Given a function $f: X \to X$ of a set $X$, then $x$ in $X$ is a periodic point if the sequence$$x, f(x), f^2(x), f^3(x), \dots$$eventually returns to $x$. (Here $f^k(x)$ denotes $k$ applications of $f$ to $x$.) The least positive $n$ such that $f^n(x) = x$ is called the period of $x$ and$$\{x, f(x), f^2(x), \dots, f^{n-1}(x)\}$$is called the orbit of $x$.
