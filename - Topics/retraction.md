## Source [^1]
- $n$. 
- (1) (in obstetrics) the state of uterine muscle fibers remaining shortened after contracting during labor. This results in a gradual progression of the fetus downwards through the pelvis. The basal portion of the uterus becomes thicker and pulls up the dilating cervix over the presenting part. 
- (2) (in dentistry) the drawing back of one or more teeth into a better position by an orthodontic appliance. 
- (3) (in dentistry) the stretching of facial tissues to expose only the teeth during dental photography.
## Source[^2]
- A retraction of a topological space $X$ to a subspace $Y \subseteq X$ is a continuous function $r:X \to Y$ such that $r(y) = y$ for all $y \in Y$, and $Y$ is referred to as a retract of $X$. Every continuous function $f:A \to Z$ can then be extended to $g:X \to Z$, where $g = f \circ r$.
