## Source [^1]
- A quadric whose equation in a suitable coordinate system is$$\frac{x^2}{a^2} - \frac{y^2}{b^2} = \frac{2z}{c}.$$
- The $yz$-plane and the $zx$-plane are planes of symmetry. Sections by planes parallel to the $xy$-plane are hyperbolas, the section by the $xy$-plane itself being a pair of straight lines. Sections by planes parallel to the other axial planes are parabolas. Planes through the $z$-axis cut the paraboloid in parabolas with vertex at the origin. The origin is a saddle-point.
