## Source [^1]
- Where a family of entities can be defined in terms of parameters, and the limiting case produces an entity which is of a different nature. For example, the general quadratic function is in the form $y = \alpha x^2 + \beta x + \gamma$, and the graph is a parabola. As $\alpha$ decreases the curvature of the parabola steadily decreases, and the limiting case as $\alpha \rightarrow 0$ is a straight line, which is a degenerate parabola.
