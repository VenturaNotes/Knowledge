## Source [^1]
- A sequence of sets $A_1, A_2, A_3, \dots$ is said to be nested and increasing if$$A_1 \subseteq A_2 \subseteq A_3 \subseteq \dots$$and is said to be nested and decreasing if$$A_1 \supseteq A_2 \supseteq A_3 \supseteq \dots.$$
