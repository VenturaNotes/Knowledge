## Source [^1]
- (of a vector on a vector) Given non-zero vectors $\mathbf{a}$ and $\mathbf{b}$, let $\overrightarrow{OA}$ and $\overrightarrow{OB}$ be directed line segments representing $\mathbf{a}$ and $\mathbf{b}$, and let $\theta$ be the angle between them ($\theta$ in radians, with $0 \le \theta \le \pi$). Let $C$ be the projection of $B$ on the line $OA$. The vector projection of $\mathbf{b}$ on $\mathbf{a}$ is the vector represented by $\overrightarrow{OC}$ The vector projection of $\mathbf{b}$ on $\mathbf{a}$ equals$$\left( \frac{\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b}}{\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{a}} \right) \mathbf{a}$$
