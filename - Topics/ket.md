## Source [^1]
- Also known as Dirac notation, the bra and ket notation highlights the difference between a wave function being thought of as a vector or a functional. So $\text{bra}(\varphi) = \langle \varphi |$ is a functional, in the dual space, acting on states $\psi$ by $\langle \varphi | : \psi \mapsto \langle \varphi, \psi \rangle$, whereas $\text{ket}(\varphi) = |\varphi \rangle$ represents a quantum state. Here $\langle ,\rangle$ denotes the inner product.
