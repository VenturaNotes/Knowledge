## Source [^1]
- A holomorphic function $f: D \to \mathbb{C}$, defined on an open (see OPEN SET) connected domain $D$ such that $|f(z)|$ has a local maximum in $D$, is constant. A similar result, known as the maximum principle, applies to harmonic functions.
