## Source [^1]
- A continuity correction applied to a chi-squared contingency table test. Normally only applied for a $2 \times 2$ table, where the effect is greatest and by a happy coincidence the arithmetic required is the simplest. It involves diminishing the numerical size of the difference between observed and expected values by 0.5 before squaring, i.e. the chi-square statistic calculated is$$\sum \frac{(|O - E| - 0.5)^2}{E}$$
