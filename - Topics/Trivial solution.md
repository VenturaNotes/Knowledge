## Source [^1]
- The zero solution of a homogeneous set of linear equations and, more generally, the zero solution for other homogeneous linear equations (or systems of equations), for example, $y = 0$ for the differential equation $dy/dx = y$. The term extends to relatively straightforward solutions for difficult equations; for example, the solutions $-2, -4, -6, \dots$ are known as the trivial solutions of the zeta function.
