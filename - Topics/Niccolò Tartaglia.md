## Source [^1]
- (1499–1557) Italian mathematician remembered as the discoverer of a method of solving cubic equations, though he may not have been the first to find a solution. His method was published by Cardano, to whom he had communicated it in confidence. Born Niccolò Fontana, his adopted nickname means 'stammerer'.
