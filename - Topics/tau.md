## Source [^1]
- The mathematical constant tau, denoted $\tau$, is defined to equal 2 times pi. Some mathematicians consider tau to be a more natural constant than pi, noting that it is the number of radians in a complete revolution and that $2\pi$ appears in many mathematical formulae such as in the pdf of the standard normal distribution, in Stirling's formula, and in Cauchy's residue theorem.
