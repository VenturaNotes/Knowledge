## Source [^1]
- A test for convergence (see CONVERGE (series)) of a series. If $\{a_n\}$ is a series which has bounded partial sums, and $\{b_n\}$ is decreasing and converges to zero, then $\sum_{1}^{\infty} a_n b_n$ converges.
