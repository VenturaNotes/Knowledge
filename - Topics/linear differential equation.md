## Source [^1]
- A differential equation of the form$$a_n y^{(n)} + a_{n-1} y^{(n-1)} + \dots + a_1 y' + a_0 y = f$$where $a_0, a_1, \dots, a_n$ and $f$ are given functions of $x$, and $y', \dots, y^{(n)}$ are derivatives of $y(x)$. See also LINEAR DIFFERENTIAL EQUATION WITH CONSTANT COEFFICIENTS, LINEAR FIRST-ORDER DIFFERENTIAL EQUATION.
