## Source [^1]
- A sequence $a_1, a_2, a_3, \dots$ is said to be decreasing if $a_i \geq a_j$ whenever $i \leq j$, and strictly decreasing if $a_i > a_j$ whenever $i < j$. See also NESTED SETS.
