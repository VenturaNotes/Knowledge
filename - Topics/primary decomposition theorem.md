## Source [^1]
- Let $p(x)$ and $q(x)$ be coprime real polynomials and $\mathbf{A}$ a square real matrix. Then$$\ker(p(\mathbf{A})q(\mathbf{A})) = \ker(p(\mathbf{A})) \oplus \ker(q(\mathbf{A})),$$where $\ker$ denotes kernel and $\oplus$ denotes a direct sum. The theorem generalizes to other fields of scalars and to linear maps on vector spaces.
