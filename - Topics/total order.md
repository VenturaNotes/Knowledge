## Source [^1]
- A partial order $\le$ on a set $X$ is a total order if it is connected; that is, for all $x, y \in X$ either $x \le y$ or $y \le x$. Well-orders are total orders; the usual order on the reals is a total order without being a well-order; divides on the positive integers is not a total order as neither of $6$ and $9$ divides the other.
