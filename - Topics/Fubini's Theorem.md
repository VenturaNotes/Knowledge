## Source [^1]
- For a Lebesgue integrable function $f: \mathbb{R}^2 \to \mathbb{R}$$$\int_{\mathbb{R}^2} f(x, y) \text{ dA} = \int_{\mathbb{R}} \left( \int_{\mathbb{R}} f(x, y) \text{ dx} \right) \text{ dy} = \int_{\mathbb{R}} \left( \int_{\mathbb{R}} f(x, y) \text{ dy} \right) \text{ dx}.$$
- This result generalizes to products of measure spaces. Compare TONELLI'S THEOREM.
