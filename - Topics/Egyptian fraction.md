## Source [^1]
- An Egyptian fraction is a sum of reciprocals of positive integers such as $\frac{1}{3} + \frac{1}{5} + \frac{1}{6}$ which sums to $\frac{7}{10}$. All positive rational numbers may be so expressed, though not uniquely, and a greedy algorithm (due to Fibonacci) will determine such expressions.
