### Fraction Rules
- $\frac{\frac ab}{c} = \frac {a}{bc}$
	- This is because $\Huge \frac{\frac ab}{c} = \frac{\frac ab}{\frac c1} = \frac ab * \frac 1c = \frac {a}{bc}$
### Examples
- $\frac{\left(\frac{3}{4}\right)}{5} = \frac{3}{20}$ 

## Source[^2]
- The fraction $a/b$, where $a$ and $b$ are positive integers, was historically obtained by dividing a unit length into $b$ parts and taking $a$ of these parts. The number $a$ is the numerator and the number $b$ is the denominator. It is a proper fraction if $a < b$ and an improper fraction if $a > b$. Any fraction can be expressed as $c + d/e$, where $c$ is an integer and $d/e$ is a proper fraction, and in this form it is called a mixed number. For example, $3\frac{1}{2}$ is a mixed fraction (equal to $7/2$). See also COMPOUND FRACTION, CONTINUED FRACTION, SIMPLE FRACTION.
