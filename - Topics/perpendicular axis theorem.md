## Source [^1]
- Suppose that a rigid body is in the form of a lamina lying in the plane $z = 0$. Let $I_x, I_y,$ and $I_z$ be the moments of inertia of the body about the three coordinate axes. The perpendicular axis theorem then states that $I_z = I_x + I_y$.
