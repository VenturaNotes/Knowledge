## Source [^1]
- (unit matrix) A diagonal matrix, symbol $I$, with each diagonal element equal to one.
## Source[^2]
- The $n \times n$ identity matrix $\mathbf{I}$, or $\mathbf{I}_n$, is the $n \times n$ matrix$$ \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0 & \dots & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 & \dots & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 & \dots & 0 \\ \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & \ddots & \vdots \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & \dots & 1 \end{bmatrix}, $$with each diagonal entry equal to 1 and all other entries equal to 0. It is the identity element for the set of $n \times n$ matrices with multiplication. See also KRONECKER DELTA.
