## Source [^1]
- A power series of the form$$F(a, b; c; x) = \sum_{k=0}^{\infty} \frac{(a)_n (b)_n}{(c)_n} \frac{x^n}{n!}$$where $(a)_n = a(a+1)(a+2)\dots(a+n-1)$ for $n \ge 1$ and $(a)_0 = 1$.
