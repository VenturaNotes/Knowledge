## Source [^1]
- Laurent’s theorem guarantees a doubly infinite series expansion$$ \sum_{n=-\infty}^{\infty} c_n(z-a)^n $$for a complex function $f(z)$ with an isolated singularity at $a$ in $\mathbb{C}$. The residue of $f(z)$ at $a$ equals $c_{-1}$. See CAUCHY’S RESIDUE THEOREM.
