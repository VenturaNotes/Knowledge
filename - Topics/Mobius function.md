## Source [^1]
- An arithmetic function important in number theory. For a natural number $n$, then $\mu(n)$ equals the sum of the primitive nth roots of unity. So $\mu(n) = 0$ if $n$ has a repeated prime factor, and otherwise $\mu(n) = 1$ or $-1$, depending on whether $n$ has an even or odd number of prime factors.
