## Source [^1]
- If $A$ and $B$ are two points on a straight line, the part of the line between and including $A$ and $B$, together with a specified direction along the line, is a directed line segment. $\vec{AB}$ denotes the directed line segment from $A$ to $B$. See also VECTOR.
