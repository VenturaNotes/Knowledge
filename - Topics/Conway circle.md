## Source [^1]
- For any triangle $ABC$, six new points can be constructed by extending each of $BA$ and $CA$ by $|BC|$, each of $AB$ and $CB$ by $|AC|$, each of $AC$ and $BC$ by $|AB|$. These six points then lie on the Conway circle. Its centre is the incenter.
