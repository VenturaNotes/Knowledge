## Source [^1]
- (difference of two sets) The set difference $A \setminus B$ of sets $A$ and $B$, also known as the relative complement, is the set consisting of all elements of $A$ that are not elements of $B$. The notation $A - B$ is also used. The set is represented by the shaded region of the Venn diagram shown in the figure.
