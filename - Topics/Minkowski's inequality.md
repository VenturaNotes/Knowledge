## Source [^1]
- Minkowski’s inequality on $\mathbb{R}^n$ states that$$\| \mathbf{x} + \mathbf{y} \|_p \le \| \mathbf{x} \|_p + \| \mathbf{y} \|_p$$where $p \ge 1$. This is the triangle inequality for the p-norm.
