## Source [^1]
- Let $T$ be a linear map between finite-dimensional vector spaces $V$ and $W$. Then the sum of the rank of $T$ and the nullity of $T$ equals the dimension of $V$. If $V$ and $W$ have equal dimensions, this implies $T$ is one-to-one if and only if $T$ is onto.
