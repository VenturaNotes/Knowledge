## Source [^1]
- Let $V_1, V_2, \dots, V_k, W$ be vector spaces over the same field. Generalizing the notion of bilinear, a multilinear map $f : V_1 \times V_2 \times \dots \times V_k \to W$ is a function $f(v_1, \dots, v_k)$ which is linear (see LINEAR MAP) in each variable $v_i$. As an example, the determinant is multilinear in its rows (and likewise in its columns).
