## Source [^1]
- (1540–1603) French mathematician who moved a step towards modern algebraic notation by using vowels to represent unknowns and consonants to represent known numbers. This practice, together with other improvements in notation, facilitated the handling of equations and enabled him to make considerable advances in algebra. He also developed the subject of trigonometry.
