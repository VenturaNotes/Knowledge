## Source [^1]
- Every polynomial equation$$f(z) = a_n z^n + a_{n-1} z^{n-1} + \dots + a_1 z + a_0 = 0,$$where the $a_i$ are real or complex numbers and $a_n \ne 0$, has a root in the set of complex numbers.
- It follows that there exist complex numbers $\alpha_1, \alpha_2, \dots, \alpha_n$ (not necessarily distinct) such that$$f(z) = a_n (z - \alpha_1)(z - \alpha_2) \dots (z - \alpha_n).$$
