## Source [^1]
- (complete quadrilateral) The configuration in a projective plane (see PROJECTIVE SPACE) consisting of four points in general position, together with the six lines joining them pairwise, as in the figure.
- ![[Pasted image 20260709233258.png|200]]
	- A complete quadrangle
- Its dual is a complete quadrilateral consisting of four lines, no three being concurrent, together with the six points in which they pairwise intersect.
