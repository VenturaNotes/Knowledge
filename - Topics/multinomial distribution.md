## Source [^1]
- The distribution of the multinomial random variable which is the generalized form of the binomial distribution to allow more than two outcomes in each of the individual trials. So if $\{Y_i\}$, $i = 1, 2, \dots, n$, is a series of independent, identically distributed random variables with $\Pr\{Y_i = x_j\} = p_j$ for $j = 1, 2, \dots, k$ with $\sum p_j = 1$ then the probabilities of the possible outcomes are given by the coefficients in the expansion of $(p_1 x_1 + p_2 x_2 + \dots + p_k x_k)^n$.
