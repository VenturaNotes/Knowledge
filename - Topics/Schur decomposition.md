## Source [^1]
- Any complex square matrix $\mathbf{A}$ can be written $\mathbf{A} = \mathbf{Q}^{-1}\mathbf{UQ}$, where $\mathbf{Q}$ is unitary and $\mathbf{U}$ is upper triangular. See also TRIANGULARIZABLE.
