## Source [^1]
- (in the plane) Let $A$ and $B$ have coordinates $(x_1, y_1)$ and $(x_2, y_2)$. It follows from Pythagoras' Theorem that the distance $|AB|$ is equal to $\sqrt{(x_2 - x_1)^2 + (y_2 - y_1)^2}$.
