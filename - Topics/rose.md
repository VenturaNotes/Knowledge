## Source [^1]
- A curve consisting of loops meeting at the origin, resembling the petals of a flower. In polar coordinates, the equation is $r = a \cos(n\theta)$, where $a > 0$. If $n$ is even, then there will be $2n$ loops, while if $n$ is odd, there are only $n$ loops.
