## Source [^1]
- For a group action of a group $G$ on a set $S$, the stabilizer of $s \in S$ consists of those $g \in G$ such that $g \cdot s=s$. This stabilizer is a subgroup of $G$. See also ORBIT-STABILIZER THEOREM.
