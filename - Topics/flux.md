## Source [^1]
- $n$. an abnormally copious flow from an organ or cavity. Alvine flux is diarrhoea.
## Source[^2]
- A measure of the strength of a field of force through a specified area. See ELECTRIC FLUX; MAGNETIC FLUX.
## Source[^3]
- Given a vector field $\mathbf{F}$ and a surface $\Sigma$ in $\mathbb{R}^3$, then the flux of $\mathbf{F}$ across $\Sigma$ is the integral $\iint_\Sigma \mathbf{F} \cdot d\mathbf{S}$. If $\mathbf{F}$ represents the velocity of a fluid, then the flux is the rate at which fluid is crossing the surface $\Sigma$. See FOURIER'S LAW, GAUSS FLUX THEOREM, SURFACE INTEGRAL.
