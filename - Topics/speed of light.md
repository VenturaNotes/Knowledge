## Source [^1]
- (symbol: $c$) The speed at which light and other electromagnetic radiation travels in a vacuum. It is a universal constant with the defined value of 299 792 458 meters per second.
## Source[^2]
- In a vacuum, light travels at $299,792,458\text{ ms}^{-1}$. This is the same for other electromagnetic waves.
