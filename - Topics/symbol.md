## Source [^1]
- (1) One of a set of distinct elements in the alphabet of a formal language. See also SIGNATURE, WORD. 
- (2) An identifier in a program.
## Source[^2]
- A single letter or sign which is used to represent an operation or relation, a function, a number, or a quantity. See NOTATION.
