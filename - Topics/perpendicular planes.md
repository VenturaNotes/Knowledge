## Source [^1]
- Two planes in 3-dimensional space are perpendicular if normals to the two planes are perpendicular. If $\mathbf{n}_1$ and $\mathbf{n}_2$ are vectors normal to the planes, this requires that $\mathbf{n}_1 \cdot \mathbf{n}_2 = 0$.
