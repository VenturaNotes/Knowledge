## Source [^1]
- A topological group $G$ is a group, which is also a topological space, such that multiplication $G \times G \to G$ given by $(g_1, g_2) \mapsto g_1 g_2$ and inversion $G \to G$ given by $g \mapsto g^{-1}$ are both continuous functions. Examples include $\mathbb{R}, \mathbb{Q}$, the circle $S^1$, and the groups listed under matrix groups. A topological group which is a (topological) manifold is necessarily a Lie group.
