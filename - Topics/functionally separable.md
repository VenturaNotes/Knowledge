## Source [^1]
- Two subsets $A$ and $B$ of a topological space $X$ are functionally separable if there exists a continuous function $f: X \to [0, 1]$ such that $f(a) = 0$ for any $a \in A$ and $f(b) = 1$ for any $b \in B$. See URYSOHN'S LEMMA.
