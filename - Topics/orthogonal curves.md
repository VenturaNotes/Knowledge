## Source [^1]
- (orthogonal surfaces) A family of curves (or surfaces) which meet at right angles whenever they intersect, for example as with the surfaces of constant $\rho, \theta, z$ in cylindrical polar coordinates. See also CURVILINEAR COORDINATES.
