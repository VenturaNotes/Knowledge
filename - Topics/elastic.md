## Source [^1]
- A variable whose elasticity with respect to another variable has absolute value greater than 1. If demand is price-elastic the proportional fall in quantity is larger than the proportional rise in price. If supply is priceelastic, an increase in price increases supply more than in proportion.
## Source[^2]
- A body is said to be elastic if, after being deformed by forces applied to it, it is able to regain its original shape as soon as the deforming forces cease to act. See DEFORMATION.
