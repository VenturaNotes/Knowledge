## Source [^1]
- (1) n. the point at which rays of light converge after passing through a lens. 
- (2) n. the principal site of an infection or other disease. 
- (3) n. (in radiography) the point of origin of an X-ray beam. 
- (4) vb. (in ophthalmology) to accommodate (see also ACCOMMODATION).
## Source[^2]
- The plural is 'foci' and the adjective is 'focal'. See CONIC, ELLIPSE, HYPERBOLA, PARABOLA.
