## Source [^1]
- The following three laws about the motion of the planets round the Sun:
	- (i) The orbit of each planet is an ellipse with the Sun at one focus.
	- (ii) The line joining a planet and the Sun sweeps out equal areas in equal times.
	- (iii) The squares of the times taken by the planets to complete an orbit are proportional to the cubes of the lengths of the major axes of their orbits.
- These laws were formulated by Kepler, based on observations, but each can be deduced from Newton's inverse square law of gravitation.
