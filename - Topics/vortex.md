## Source [^1]
- A region of fluid revolving around an axis in three dimensions or a point in the plane, such as arises in whirlpools and cyclones. A line vortex at the origin has flow velocity$$\mathbf{u}(x, y) = \frac{\Gamma}{2\pi} \left( \frac{-y}{x^2 + y^2}, \frac{x}{x^2 + y^2} \right).$$
- The flow has circulation $\Gamma$ but is, perhaps surprisingly, irrotational, which highlights that curl measures local rather than global rotation. For a diagram see SINGULARITY (FLUID DYNAMICS).
