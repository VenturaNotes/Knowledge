## Source [^1]
- A transformation in which points move parallel to a fixed line or plane and move a distance proportional to their distance from the fixed line or plane. Areas and volumes are preserved under a shear in two and three dimensions respectively. When the fixed line or plane is parallel to one of the sides, a shear transforms rectangles into parallelograms and cuboids into parallelepipeds.
