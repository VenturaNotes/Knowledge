## Source [^1]
- A ring formed by the cosets of an ideal. Let $R/I$ denote the set of cosets of an ideal $I$ of a ring $R$. The ring operations on $R$ induce a well-defined ring operation on $R/I$ given by $(r_1 + I) + (r_2 + I) = (r_1 + r_2) + I$ and $(r_1 + I)(r_2 + I) = r_1r_2 + I$.
