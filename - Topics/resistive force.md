## Source [^1]
- A force that opposes the motion of a body. It acts on the body in a direction opposite to the direction of the velocity of the body. Examples include friction and aerodynamic drag. When there is a resistive force, the principle of conservation of energy does not hold, but the work–energy principle does hold.
