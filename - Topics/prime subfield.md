## Source [^1]
- The smallest subfield of a field. For characteristic zero fields, the prime subfield is isomorphic to $\mathbb{Q}$, and for characteristic $p$ fields the prime subfield is isomorphic to $\mathbb{Z}_p$ the integers modulo $p$.
