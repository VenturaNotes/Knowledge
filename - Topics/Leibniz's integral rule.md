## Source [^1]
- The following rule for differentiating integrals:$$\frac{d}{dt} \int_{a(t)}^{b(t)} f(x, t) \, dx = f(b(t), t)b'(t) - f(a(t), t)a'(t) + \int_{a(t)}^{b(t)} \frac{\partial f}{\partial t}(x, t) \, dx.$$
- If $a(t)$ and $b(t)$ are constant, then this amounts to differentiation under the integral sign. Compare REYNOLD’S TRANSPORT THEOREM.
