## Source [^1]
- A number is said to be in scientific notation (or standard form) when it is written as $a \times 10^n$, where $1 \le a < 10$ and $n$ is an integer. Thus $634.8$ and $0.00234$ are written in scientific notation as $6.348 \times 10^2$ and $2.34 \times 10^{-3}$. The notation is particularly useful for very large and very small numbers. See also ORDER OF MAGNITUDE.
