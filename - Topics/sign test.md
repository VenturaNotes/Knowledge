## Source [^1]
- A non-parametric method to test the null hypothesis that a sample is selected from a population with median $m$. If the null hypothesis is true, the sample of size $n$ is expected to have an equal number of observations above and below $m$, and the probability that $r$ of them are greater than $m$ has the binomial distribution $B(n, 0.5)$. The null hypothesis is rejected if the value lies in the critical region determined by the chosen significance level.
- The test may also be used to compare the medians of two populations from paired data.
