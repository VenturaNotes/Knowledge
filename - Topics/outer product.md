## Source [^1]
- Given an $m \times 1$ column vector $\mathbf{v}$ and $n \times 1$ column vector $\mathbf{w}$, their outer product is $\mathbf{v} \mathbf{w}^T$, which is an $m \times n$ matrix. The outer product is written as $\mathbf{v} \otimes \mathbf{w}$, as it is equivalent to the tensor product.
