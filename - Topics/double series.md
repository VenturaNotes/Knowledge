## Source [^1]
- A series with two indices. For example, for any joint distribution with $p_{ij} = \text{Pr}\{X = x_i, Y = y_j\}$, $E(X+Y) = \sum_i \sum_j ((x_i + y_j) \times p_{ij})$.
