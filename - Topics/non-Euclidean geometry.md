## Source [^1]
- After unsuccessful attempts had been made at proving that the parallel postulate could be deduced from the other postulates of Euclid's, the matter was settled by the discovery of non-Euclidean geometries by Lobachevsky and Bolyai. In these, all Euclid's postulates hold except the parallel postulate. In hyperbolic geometry, given a point not on a given line, there are at least two lines through the point parallel to the line (i.e. these parallel lines do not intersect the given line). In elliptic geometry, given a point not on a given line, there are no parallels through the point. See ELLIPTIC PLANE, HYPERBOLIC PLANE, NEUTRAL GEOMETRY.
