## Source [^1]
- (geometric progression) A real or complex, finite or infinite sequence $a_1, a_2, a_3, \dots$ with a common ratio $r$, so that $a_2/a_1 = r, a_3/a_2 = r, \dots$ The first term is usually denoted by $a$. For example, $3, 6, 12, 24, 48, \dots$ is the geometric sequence with $a = 3, r = 2$. In such a geometric sequence, the $n$th term $a_n$ is given by $a_n = ar^{n-1}$.
