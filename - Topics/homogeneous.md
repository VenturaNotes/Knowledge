## Source [^1]
- (statistics) Literally meaning ‘of the same kind or nature’. A homogeneous population is one where the members are similar in nature. If this is not the case, then it may be more appropriate to use some form of representative sampling, such as quota or stratified.
---
- (Markov chains) A Markov chain $X_1, X_2, X_3, \dots$ is homogeneous if the transition matrix from $X_n$ to $X_{n+1}$ is constant, that is, independent of $n$.
