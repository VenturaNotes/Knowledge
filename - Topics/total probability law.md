## Source [^1]
- Let $A_1, A_2, \dots, A_n$ be mutually exclusive events whose union is the whole sample space of an experiment. Then the total probability law is the following formula for the probability of an event $B$:$$\Pr(B) = \Pr(B|A_1)\Pr(A_1) + \Pr(B|A_2)\Pr(A_2) + \dots + \Pr(B|A_n)\Pr(A_n).$$
