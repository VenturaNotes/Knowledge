## Source [^1]
- If $l$ is a diameter of a central conic, the midpoints of the chords parallel to $l$ lie on a straight line $l'$, which is also a diameter. The midpoints of the chords parallel to $l'$ lie on $l$. Then $l$ and $l'$ are conjugate diameters. In the case of a circle, conjugate diameters are perpendicular.
