## Source [^1]
- To change a measurement system to one based on multiples of 10. In 1971 the British currency was decimalized from $\textsterling 1 = 20$ shillings and 1 shilling $= 12$ pence to $\textsterling 1 = 100$ (new) pence (the 'new' has since been dropped).
