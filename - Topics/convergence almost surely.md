## Source [^1]
- A sequence of random variables $X_n$ converges almost surely to a random variable $X$ if$$\text{Pr}(\lim_{n \to \infty} X_n = X) = 1.$$
- This is written $X_n \xrightarrow{a.s} X$ and is also known as strong convergence. Almost sure convergence implies convergence in probability. See STRONG LAW OF LARGE NUMBERS.
