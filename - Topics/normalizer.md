## Source [^1]
- Given a group $G$ with subgroup $H$, the normalizer $N_G(H)$ of $H$ in $G$ equals

$$
N_G(H) = \{g \in G \mid gH = Hg\}.
$$
- Then $N_G(H)$ is a subgroup of $G$ which contains $H$ as a normal subgroup. Indeed, $N_G(H)$ is the largest subgroup containing $H$ as a normal subgroup. Compare CENTRALIZER.
