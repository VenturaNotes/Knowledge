## Source [^1]
- (rule of false position, regula falsi) An iterative method which usually converges faster than the bisection method. Say that $f(a) > 0 > f(b)$ and we wish to approximate a root of $f$. The first iterate is $x_1 = (af(b)-bf(a))/(f(b)-f(a))$; this is the $x$-value where the line connecting $(a, f(a))$ and $(b, f(b))$ crosses the $x$-axis. Depending on the sign of $f(x_1)$ this process is repeated on the interval $[a, x_1]$ or $[x_1, b]$ to find $x_2$ and so on.
