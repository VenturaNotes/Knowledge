## Source [^1]
- For two vector spaces $V, W$ over a field $F$, then $\operatorname{Hom}(V, W)$ denotes the space of linear maps from $V$ to $W$. Its dimension equals $\dim V \times \dim W$. Note that $\operatorname{Hom}(V, V)$ is the endomorphism ring $\operatorname{End}(V)$ and that $\operatorname{Hom}(V, F)$ is the dual space of $V$.
- The notation is more generally used; for example $\operatorname{Hom}(G, H)$, the additive group of homomorphisms where $G, H$ are abelian groups, or to denote $\operatorname{Hom}$ functors in category theory.
