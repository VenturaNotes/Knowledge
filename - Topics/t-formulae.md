## Source [^1]
- A range of trigonometric identities that are useful as substitutions for integrations, often as half-angle formulae. If $t = \tan \frac{1}{2} A$, then$$\sin A = \frac{2t}{1+t^2}, \quad \cos A = \frac{1-t^2}{1+t^2}, \quad \tan A = \frac{2t}{1-t^2}.$$
