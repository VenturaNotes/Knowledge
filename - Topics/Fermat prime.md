## Source [^1]
- A prime of the form $2^{2^r} + 1$. Fermat conjectured all such numbers to be prime, though Euler showed that $2^{32} + 1$ is divisible by 641. Currently, the only known Fermat primes are for $r \le 4$. See also CONSTRUCTIBLE POINT.
