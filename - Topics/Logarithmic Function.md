## Source [^1]
- ($\ln$) The logarithmic function $\ln x$ may be defined for positive $x$ as follows:$$\ln x = \int_1^x \frac{1}{t} \, dt$$
- ![[Pasted image 20260909030229.png|255]]

- It can equally be defined as the inverse function of the exponential function. $\ln x$ is differentiable and increasing with derivative $1/x$.

- Further, $\ln x = \log_e x$ satisfies properties (i)–(v) listed under logarithm.
