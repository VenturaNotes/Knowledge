## Source [^1]
- (Frobenius normal form) The rational canonical form, or Frobenius normal form, of a square matrix is a block diagonal matrix which is similar to the matrix and where the blocks are companion matrices. The blocks are $C(d_1), C(d_2), \dots, C(d_k)$, where $d_1, d_2, \dots, d_k$ are monic polynomials such that $d_i$ divides $d_{i+1}$ and $C(d_i)$ denotes the companion matrix of $d_i$. The minimal polynomial equals $d_k$ and the characteristic polynomial equals the product $d_1d_2 \dots d_k$. See JORDAN NORMAL FORM, STRUCTURE THEOREM (for modules).
