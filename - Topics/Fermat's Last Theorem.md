## Source [^1]
- The statement that, for all integers $n > 2$, the equation $x^n + y^n = z^n$ has no solution in positive integers. Fermat famously noted in a copy of Diophantus' Arithmetica that he had a proof 'too large fit in the margin'. Much research was done over centuries until a proof was completed in 1995 by Andrew Wiles. See PYTHAGOREAN TRIPLE, MODULARITY THEOREM.
