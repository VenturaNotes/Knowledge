## Source [^1]
- The power series$$x - \frac{x^2}{2} + \frac{x^3}{3} - \dots + (-1)^{n+1} \frac{x^n}{n} + \dots$$
- For $-1 < x \leq 1$ the series converges to $\ln(1 + x)$. For $x$ a complex number, with $|x| \leq 1$ and $x \neq -1$, the series converges to a branch of $\log(1 + x)$, where $\log$ denotes the complex logarithm.
