## Source [^1]
- An entry in a set of statistical data which lies a long way from any pattern apparent in the rest of the data set. The presence of an outlier suggests two possibilities which warrant further investigation. One is that something really exceptional occurred to cause it; such an occurrence is a shock. The other possibility is that the outlier occurred because of a mistake in recording or processing the data. See also INLIER.
## Source[^2]
- $n$. In statistics, an extreme score that is widely separated from the rest of the scores and raises the possibility of an error in measurement, recording, or data entry.
## Source[^3]
- An observation that is deemed to be unusual and possibly erroneous because it does not follow the general pattern of the data in the sample. However, in some contexts it is the outliers which are the most important observations. See EXTREME VALUE DISTRIBUTION.
