## Source [^1]
- Poisson's solution to the Dirichlet problem in the upper half-plane, $\nabla^2 V = 0$ for $y > 0$ and $V(x, 0) = f(x)$, is given by the integral formula

$$V(x, y) = \frac{y}{\pi} \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} \frac{f(t) \, dt}{(t - x)^2 + y^2}.$$
