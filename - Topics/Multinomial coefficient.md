## Source [^1]
- The number$$ \binom{n}{n_1\quad n_2\quad \dots n_k} = \frac{n!}{n_1! n_2! \dots n_k!} $$when $n_1 + n_2 + \dots + n_k = n$.
- This is the coefficient of $x_1^{n_1} x_2^{n_2} \dots x_k^{n_k}$ in the expansion of $(x_1 + x_2 + \dots + x_k)^n$. It also gives the number of ways of choosing $n_i$ of each of $k$ types when choosing a total of $n$ objects, without regard to the order in which they are chosen.
