## Source [^1]
- (1804–51) German mathematician responsible for notable developments in the theory of elliptic functions, a class of functions defined to invert elliptic integrals. Applying them to number theory, he was able to reprove Lagrange's Theorem that every integer is the sum of four perfect squares, showing further in how many different ways.
