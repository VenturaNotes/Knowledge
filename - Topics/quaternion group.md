## Source [^1]
- The group $Q_8$ of quaternions $\{\pm 1, \pm i, \pm j, \pm k\}$ under multiplication. It has a presentation$$Q_8 = \{x, y : x^4 = 1, x^2 = y^2, y^{-1}xy = x^{-1}\}.$$
