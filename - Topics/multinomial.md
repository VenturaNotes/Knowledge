## Source [^1]
- The general description for an algebraic expression that is a sum of two or more terms. However, where powers of variables, such as in $3x^4 + x^2 - x + 1$, are involved, ‘polynomial’ is more common, and for two or three terms ‘binomial’ and ‘trinomial’ are more typically used.
