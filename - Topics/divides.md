## Source [^1]
- Let $a$ and $b$ be integers. Then $a$ divides $b$, written $a|b$, if there is an integer $c$ such that $ac = b$. It is said that $a$ is a divisor or factor of $b$, that $b$ is divisible by $a$, and that $b$ is a multiple of $a$.
