### Examples 
- When finding limits
	- $\infty - 1$ = $\infty$
	- $\infty$ $\cdot$ $\infty$ = $\infty$
	- $\frac {1}{e^\infty} = 0$ [^2]
		- Any number divided by $\infty$ equals 0
## Source[^3]
- For the real numbers, a value greater than any finite number, denoted by $\infty$. It is natural to introduce notions of tending to $\infty$ and $-\infty$, though these are not themselves real numbers. For the complex numbers, it is natural to introduce a notion of a single infinity $\infty$ to make the extended complex plane.
