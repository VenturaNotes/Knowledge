- In mechanics, the position vector of a particle is often denoted by $\mathbf{r}$, which is a function of the time $t$, giving the position of the particle as it moves. If Cartesian coordinates are used, then $\mathbf{r} = x\mathbf{i} + y\mathbf{j} + z\mathbf{k}$, where $x, y,$ and $z$ are functions of $t$.
