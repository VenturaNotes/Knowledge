## Source [^1]
- A square complex matrix $U$ such that $UU^* = I = U^*U$, where $U^* = \bar{U}^T$ denotes the Hermitian conjugate of $U$. The $n \times n$ unitary matrices are the matrices which preserve the norm on $\mathbb{C}^n$. Compare ORTHOGONAL MATRIX.
