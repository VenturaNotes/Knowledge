## Source [^1]
- $n$. the cavity in the skull that contains the eye. It is formed from parts of the frontal, sphenoid, zygomatic, lacrimal, ethmoid, palatine, and maxillary bones. $\textendash$orbital adj.
## Source[^2]
- (in anatomy) Either of the two sockets in the skull of vertebrates that house the eyeballs.
## Source[^3]
### Mechanics
- The path traced out by a body experiencing a central force, such as the gravitational force associated with a second body. When the force is given by the inverse square law of gravitation, the orbit is either elliptic, parabolic, or hyperbolic, with the second body at a focus of the conic.
### Group Theory
- For a group action $G$ on a set $S$, the orbit of $s \in S$ is the subset $\{g \cdot s \mid g \in G\}$. The orbits partition the set $S$.
