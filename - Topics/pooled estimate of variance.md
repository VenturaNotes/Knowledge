## Source [^1]
- (standard deviation) If it is reasonable to assume that the variances of two populations that you wish to compare are equal, then the most discriminatory test available uses a single estimate of the common variance and uses all of the available data from the two samples. The pooled estimate $s_p$ is given by$$s_p^2 = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^{n_X} (x_i - \bar{x})^2 + \sum_{j=1}^{n_Y} (y_j - \bar{y})^2}{n_X + n_Y - 2}$$where $\{x_i\}, \{y_j\}$ are the two sets of sample observations with sample sizes $n_X, n_Y$ respectively and sample means $\bar{x}, \bar{y}$.
