## Source [^1]
- Any of the following real functions: the rational functions, the trigonometric functions, the logarithmic and exponential functions, the functions $f$ defined by $f(x) = x^{m/n}$ (where $m$ and $n$ are non-zero integers), and all those functions that can be obtained from these by using addition, subtraction, multiplication, division, composition, and the taking of inverse functions. Compare SPECIAL FUNCTION.
