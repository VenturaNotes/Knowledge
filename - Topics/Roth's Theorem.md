## Source [^1]
- A result in number theory relating to the approximation of algebraic numbers by rational numbers. For an irrational algebraic number $\alpha$ and any $\varepsilon > 0$, there are finitely many rationals $p/q$ (in simplest form) such that$$|\alpha - \frac{p}{q}| < \frac{1}{q^{2+\varepsilon}}.$$
- The theorem is false for $\varepsilon = 0$ by Dirichlet's approximation theorem.
