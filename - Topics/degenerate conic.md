## Source [^1]
- A conic that consists of a pair of (possibly coincident) straight lines. The equation $ax^2 + 2hxy + by^2 + 2gx + 2fy + c = 0$ represents a degenerate conic if $\Delta = 0$, where$$\Delta = \begin{vmatrix} a & h & g \\ h & b & f \\ g & f & c \end{vmatrix}.$$
