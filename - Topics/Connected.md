## Source [^1]
- (of a relation) A binary relation $\sim$ is connected if for all pairs of distinct elements $x,y,$ either $x \sim y$ or $y \sim x$. So, for example, in the set of real numbers the relation 'is greater than' is connected. 
---
- (of a space) A topological space (or metric space) is connected if it cannot be partitioned into two non-empty open subsets (see OPEN SET). The continuous image (see CONTINUOUS FUNCTION) of a connected space is connected. The product of two connected spaces is connected. Path-connected spaces are connected, but the converse is not true.
