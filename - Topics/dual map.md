## Source [^1]
- For a linear map $T: V \to W$ of vector spaces, the dual map $T^*: W^* \to V^*$ between the dual spaces is defined by $(T^* \varphi)(v) = \varphi(Tv)$ for $\varphi$ in $W^*$ and $v$ in $V$. If the matrix for $T$ is $M$, with respect to bases for $V$ and $W$, then the matrix for $T^*$ is the transpose $M^T$ with respect to the dual bases.
