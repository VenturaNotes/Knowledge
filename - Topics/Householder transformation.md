## Source [^1]
- (Householder reflection) Named after Alston Householder (1904–93), a Householder transformation is a reflection in a hyperplane in $\mathbb{R}^n$. A hyperplane can be characterized by a unit normal $\mathbf{u}$, in which case the Householder transformation is$$\mathbf{x} \mapsto \mathbf{x} - 2(\mathbf{x} \cdot \mathbf{u})\mathbf{u}.$$
- Householder transformations play a key role in finding the QR decomposition.
