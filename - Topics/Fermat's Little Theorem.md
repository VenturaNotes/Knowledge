## Source [^1]
- Let $p$ be a prime and $a$ be an integer not divisible by $p$. Then $a^{p-1} \equiv 1 \pmod{p}$. Consequently, for $a$ any integer, $a^p \equiv a \pmod{p}$. See also TOTIENT FUNCTION.
