## Source [^1]
- The square root of the ratio of a moment of inertia to the mass of a rigid body. Thus, if $I$ is the moment of inertia of a rigid body of mass $m$ about a specified axis, and $k$ is the radius of gyration, then $I = mk^2$. It means that, when rotating about this axis, the body has the same moment of inertia as a ring of the same mass and of radius $k$.
