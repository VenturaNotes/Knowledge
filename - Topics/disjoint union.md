## Source [^1]
- A union where duplicate elements from two sets are separately included (which is not the case with standard union). For example, given two sets $A_1, A_2$ their disjoint union, denoted $A_1 \sqcup A_2$, may be identified with $(A_1 \times \{1\}) \cup (A_2 \times \{2\})$. If $x$ is common to both $A_1$ and $A_2$, its first version appears as $(x, 1)$ in $A_1 \sqcup A_2$ and the second copy as $(x, 2)$.
