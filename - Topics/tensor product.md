## Source [^1]
- Given vector spaces $V$ and $W$ (over the same field $F$), with bases $v_1, \dots, v_m$ and $w_1, \dots, w_n$ respectively, the tensor product $V \otimes W$ may be defined as the vector space spanned by the formal symbols $v_i \otimes w_j$. This then defines a vector space of dimension $mn$.
- More naturally, $V \otimes W$ may be defined without reference to bases, as the dual space of the space of bilinear maps $B : V \times W \to F$ via $(v \otimes w)(B) = B(v, w)$. Further, $\text{Hom}(V, W)$ is then isomorphic to $V^* \otimes W$ via $(f \otimes w)(v) = f(v)w$.
