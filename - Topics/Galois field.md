## Source [^1]
- A finite field. The order of a finite field is necessarily $p^n$, where $p$ is a prime, and for each such order there is a finite field which is unique up to isomorphism. The Galois field of order $q$ is denoted $\mathbb{F}_q$ and $\mathbb{F}_p$ is $\mathbb{Z}_p$.
