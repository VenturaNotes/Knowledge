## Source [^1]
- In a topological space $X$, two sets where each set is disjoint from the closure of the other. Two separated sets are then necessarily disjoint, but the sets $[0, 1)$ and $(1, 2]$ are disjoint, but not separated, as $1$ belongs to both closures. See SEPARATION AXIOMS.
