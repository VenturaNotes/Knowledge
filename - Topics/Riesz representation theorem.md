## Source [^1]
- Given a finite-dimensional inner product space $V$ and a linear functional $\varphi \in V'$, there exists $w \in V$ such that $\varphi(v) = \langle v, w \rangle$ for all $v \in V$. Further the norm $\|\varphi\|$ of $\varphi$ equals the norm of $w$. The theorem extends to infinite-dimensional Hilbert spaces and their (analytic) dual spaces.
