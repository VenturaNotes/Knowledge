## Source [^1]
- The sign $\sqrt{ }$ used in connection with square roots, cube roots, and, more generally, nth roots for larger values of $n$. The notation $\sqrt{a}$ indicates square root, $\sqrt[3]{a}$ indicates cube root and $\sqrt[n]{a}$ indicates $n$th root of $a$. For a detailed explanation of the correct usage of the notation, see NTH ROOT, SQUARE ROOT. See also SOLVABLE BY RADICALS.
