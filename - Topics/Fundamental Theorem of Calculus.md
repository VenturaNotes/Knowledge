## Source [^1]
- This theorem makes explicit how integration is the inverse of differentiation.
- If $f$ is a continuous function on $[a,b]$ and $F$ is an antiderivative of $f$, so that $F'(x)=f(x)$ for all $x$ in $[a,b]$, then$$\int_a^b f(x) \, dx = F(b) - F(a).$$
- It follows that$$\frac{d}{dx} \int_a^x f(t) \, dt = f(x).$$
