## Source [^1]
- (of a function) Let $f$ be a function that is integrable on the closed interval $[a, b]$. The mean value of $f$ over the interval $[a, b]$ is equal to$$\frac{1}{b-a} \int_{a}^{b} f(x) dx.$$
- The mean value $\bar{y}$ has the property that the area under the curve $y=f(x)$ between $x=a$ and $x=b$ is equal to the area under the horizontal line $y=\bar{y}$ between $x=a$ and $x=b$, which is the area of a rectangle with width $b-a$ and height $\bar{y}$.
