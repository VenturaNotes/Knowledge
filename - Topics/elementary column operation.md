## Source [^1]
- Commonly abbreviated to ECO. One of the following operations on the columns of a matrix:
	- (i) interchange two columns,
	- (ii) multiply a column by a non-zero scalar,
	- (iii) add a multiple of one column to another column.
- An elementary column operation can be produced by post-multiplication by the appropriate elementary matrix.
