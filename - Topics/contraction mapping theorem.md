## Source [^1]
- If $f$ is a contraction mapping on a non-empty, complete metric space $X$, then there is a unique fixed point $x$ in $X$ under the mapping, i.e. $f(x) = x$ for exactly one point $x$. This fixed point is the limit of the Cauchy sequence $x_{n+1} = f(x_n)$ beginning from any $x_1$.
