## Source [^1]
- The branch of mathematics that seeks to model financial markets valuing stock derivatives (especially options) which mainly uses methods of stochastic calculus. Following the financial crisis of 2008, the credibility of such models has been damaged, though criticism dates back to Mandelbrot. See BLACK-SCHOLES EQUATION, STOCHASTIC DIFFERENTIAL EQUATION.
