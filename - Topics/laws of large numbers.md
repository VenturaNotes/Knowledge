## Source [^1]
- (in statistics) The generic name given to a group of theorems relating to the behaviour of $\frac{S_n}{n} = \frac{1}{n} (X_1 + X_2 \dots + X_n)$ for large $n$, where $\{X_i\}$ are identically distributed, independent random variables, usually identically distributed. See CENTRAL LIMIT THEOREM, STRONG LAW OF LARGE NUMBERS, WEAK LAW OF LARGE NUMBERS.
