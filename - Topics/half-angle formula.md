## Source [^1]
- Trigonometric formulae expressing a function of a half-angle in terms of the full angle or expressing a function of an angle in terms of half-angles.$$\begin{align} &\sin \frac{x}{2} = \pm \sqrt{\frac{1 - \cos x}{2}}, \cos \frac{x}{2} = \pm \sqrt{\frac{1 + \cos x}{2}}, \\&\text{and if }t=tan\frac x2, sin~x=\frac{2t}{1+t^2},\\&cosx=\frac{1-t^2}{1+t^2},tan\quad x=\frac{2t}{1-t^2}.\end{align}$$
