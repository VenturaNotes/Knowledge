## Source [^1]
- Given a basis $e_1, e_2, \dots, e_n$ for a vector space $V$, the functionals $f_1, f_2, \dots, f_n$ defined by $f_i(e_j) = \delta_{ij}$ (the Kronecker delta) form a basis for the dual space $V^*$. For $v$ in $V, f_i(v)$ is the $i$th coordinate of $v$ with respect to $e_1, e_2, \dots, e_n$.
