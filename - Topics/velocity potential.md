## Source [^1]
- For an inviscid, irrotational flow, with flow velocity $\mathbf{u}$ (in a simply connected region) a velocity potential is a scalar function $\varphi$ such that $\nabla\varphi = \mathbf{u}$. The velocity potential is unique up to a constant. See COMPLEX POTENTIAL; STREAM FUNCTION.
