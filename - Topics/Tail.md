## Source [^1]
- (of a list) (1) The last item in a list. 
- (2) The list remaining when the head has been removed.
## Source[^2]
- A tail of a sequence $(x_n)_{n=1}^\infty$ is a subsequence of the form $(x_n)_{n=N}^\infty$ for some $N$; that is, it is the remainder of the sequence past some given index. A sequence converges to a limit $l$ if every neighborhood of $l$ contains a tail of the sequence.
