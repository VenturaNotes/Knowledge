## Source [^1]
- Let $\alpha$ be a real number and $Q > 1$ be an integer. Then there are integers $p, q$ with $0 < q < Q$ such that $|q\alpha - p| \le 1/Q$. Consequently, there are infinitely many $p, q$ satisfying $|\alpha - p/q| \le 1/q^2$. This is a significant result in Diophantine approximation, the study of approximating real numbers by rational numbers. Compare ROTH'S THEOREM.
