## Source [^1]
- The energy $E$ of a photon equals $hv$, where $h$ is Planck’s constant and $v$ denotes the light’s frequency. This alternatively is written $E = \hbar\omega$, where $\hbar = h/(2\pi)$ is the reduced Planck constant and $\omega$ denotes angular frequency. Its approximate value is $6.6 \times 10^{-34}$ Js. The constant appears in Schrödinger’s equation and Heisenberg’s Uncertainty Principle.
