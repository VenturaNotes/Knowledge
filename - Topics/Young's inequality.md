## Source [^1]
- Given $x, y > 0$ and $a, b > 1$ such that $1/a + 1/b = 1$, then$$xy \leq \frac{x^a}{a} + \frac{y^b}{b}.$$
- When $a = b = 2$, this is equivalent to the arithmetic-geometric mean inequality.
