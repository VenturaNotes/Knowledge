## Source [^1]
- In coordinate geometry of the plane, a useful necessary and sufficient condition that two lines, with gradients $m_1$ and $m_2$, are perpendicular is that $m_1 m_2 = -1$. (This is taken to include the cases when $m_1 = 0$ and $m_2$ is infinite, and vice versa.)
